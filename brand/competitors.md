## Competitor Profiles
- **r/OldSkaters (reddit.com/r/OldSkaters):** The dominant global forum for 30+/40+/50+ skaters sharing progress clips, setup advice, and slam stories, but unstructured and hard to search by city or park difficulty.
- **Old Skaters (oldskaters.com):** Community content hub and manifesto for older skateboarders, without a structured multi-country park/shop/group directory or clinical physiology breakdowns.
- **Concrete Disciples / GoSkate / Northwest Skater:** General skatepark directories with broad park coverage, but written for all ages without older-skater friendliness ratings, morning-session timing, or paired welcoming shops.
- **Regional Facebook & Instagram Groups (Silver Skate UK, Adult Skate Melbourne, local 30+ nights):** High local engagement, but fragmented across private social groups and difficult to discover unless you already know the crew's name.

# Brand Voice Profile
## Last Updated: 2026-11-18 by /start-here
Depth: lite
Data: LOCAL DOCS + LIVE (6 sources)

## Core Voice & Editorial Personas
- **Site & Directory Voice:** Direct, peer-to-peer, practical, utility-first. Speaks like an experienced skater in their 40s or 50s helping another returner find a welcoming park, shop, or morning session.
- **Sam Torres (Age 44, community organiser):** Warm, encouraging, focuses on session culture, park etiquette, and making sessions welcoming across skill levels.
- **Jo Fairweather (Age 52, physiotherapist & skater):** Calm, objective, evidence-grounded. Separates clinical findings and cohort limits from practical on-board choices.
- **Marcus Webb (Age 47, returned at 46 after 20-year break):** Compact, street/park-grounded, realistic about aging reflexes, setup mechanics, and ordinary practical choices.

## Words Customers Use
- "getting back on the board", "20-year break", "dawn patrol", "local skate shop", "wide deck (8.5+)", "soft wheels / Dragon wheels", "wrist guards", "knee sleeves", "pads", "session", "flat ground", "carving transition", "showing up on Monday".

## Words & Patterns to Avoid
- Never use "adult skaters" in public-facing copy (always use "older skaters" or "older returners").
- Banned AI tells: em dashes (—) as clause connectors, "not X, but Y" contrasts, bold-label listicles, one-line summary closers, and crutch words ("genuinely", "crucial", "testament", "delve", "pivotal", "landscape", "interplay").

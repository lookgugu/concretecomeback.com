# Positioning
## Last Updated: 2026-11-18 by /start-here
Depth: lite
Data: LOCAL DOCS + LIVE (6 sources)

## Current Promise
- **Hero headline:** "You Never Forgot How to Ride. Find skate parks welcoming to older skaters, helpful local shops, and communities of peers getting back on a board."
- **Trust stance:** 100% free community resource across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Manually curated listings, zero paid placements, zero affiliate links.
- **Newsletter CTA:** "Weekly roundup: Keep your comeback rolling. The week's best new reads and, when available, one practical idea for your weekend session."

## What Readers Value Most
- Specific, zero-judgment intelligence they cannot get from Google Maps or youth skate media: which parks have mellow transitions and quiet morning hours, which local shops welcome a 45-year-old buying pads and an 8.5" setup, and realistic physiology/injury guidance.

## The Gap
- **Directory vs. Newsletter Disconnect:** The site draws search visitors with specific problems (setup, injury prevention, local parks), but the email signup pitches a generic "Weekly roundup" ("the week's best new reads"). Over a 30-day window (~1,400 visitors), newsletter submissions were 0.
- **Shop & Group Density vs. Parks:** 279 skate parks are listed, compared to only 38 shops and 22 community groups, leaving most park pages without a paired local shop or over-35 meetup.
- **Topic Cannibalization:** 193 blog posts include 4 overlapping clusters (11 posts competing on how to fall safely, six-month plateau, getting back on board, and recovery days).

## Chosen Angle
- **★ The "Monday-Ready Session" Angle:** Practical, evidence-grounded guidance and vetted local park/shop/group intelligence built specifically for older skaters (35–60+) who want to progress on the weekend and still show up to work healthy on Monday.

## Editorial & Brand Guardrails
- User-facing copy must say "older skaters" or "older returners", never "adult" (URL slugs and data attributes retain their historical keys).
- No paid placements, no affiliate links, no sales pitches on directory listings.
- Every blog post is assigned to an established author persona (Sam Torres, Jo Fairweather, Marcus Webb, or Concrete Comeback Editorial Team) and never invents biographical claims, witnessed sessions, or patient consults.
- Follow the Anti-AI Slop Rubric: no em dashes as universal connectors, no formulaic "Not X, but Y" pivots, no repetitive bold-label listicles, no dramatic one-line closers, and no model crutch words ("genuinely", "pivotal", "testament", "delve", "crucial", "landscape", "interplay", "rich tapestry").
- Never write a newsletter signup CTA into blog post Markdown (BlogLayout renders the inline signup form automatically).

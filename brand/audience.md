# Audience Profile
## Last Updated: 2026-11-18 by /start-here
Depth: lite
Data: LOCAL DOCS + LIVE (6 sources)

## Segments
- **Returning skaters (35–60+):** People in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia who skated in the 70s, 80s, or 90s, stepped away for 15–30 years for careers, kids, and joints, and are getting back on a board.
- **Older beginners & skate parents (35–55):** Parents taking their kids to the skatepark who decide to learn alongside them and want low-ego sessions and welcoming instruction.

## Desires & Buying Triggers
- **Core Desires:** Find a skatepark with mellow transitions, smooth concrete, and quiet morning hours where an older skater in full pads won't feel out of place; find an independent local skate shop whose staff won't talk down to a 45-year-old asking about wider decks (8.25"–9.0"+), forgiving wheels, and protective gear; connect with local 30+/40+ skate nights or Sunday morning crews; learn how to warm up, fall on pads, and manage knee, hip, achilles, and lower-back load so they can still go to work on Monday.
- **Triggers:** Dusting off an old board in the garage or taking a child to a local skatepark; taking a first slam back and realizing 45-year-old wrists and knees need real pads, softer wheels, or fall mechanics; moving past driveway flatground and wanting a non-intimidating park or peer group nearby.

## Dominant Emotion & Sophistication
- **Dominant Emotion:** Cautious excitement mixed with imposter syndrome at the park and practical fear of a work-disrupting injury (wrist fracture, knee sprain, achilles strain).
- **Market Sophistication:** High skepticism toward youth-focused trick tutorials, hype gear marketing, and generic fitness advice; high trust in specific hardware specs (durometer, wheelbase, truck geometry), clinical biomechanics, and peer-tested session advice.

## Watering Holes
- `r/OldSkaters` on Reddit (primary global community for 30+/40+/50+ skaters)
- Local Facebook & Instagram 30+/40+ skate groups (Silver Skate UK, Adult Skate Melbourne, Silver Skaters Austin, Old Man Mondays)
- Independent local skate shops and weekday/Sunday dawn-patrol park sessions
- YouTube channels focused on transition carving, setup mechanics, and older-skater progression

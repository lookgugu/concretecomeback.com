The bowls here are described by Skateboard Scotland as mellow, which is the word you want when your transition skills are still coming back. The quarters share a spine, and the street side is stairs, square flatbars, down rails and ledges rather than anything gappy.

The history is the bonus. Kelvin Wheelies opened on this spot in 1978 — one of the second wave of 1970s concrete parks, with a 50-metre slalom run feeding the "Torpedo" and "Bazooka" bowls — and its remains are still visible in the grass beside the modern park. In 2025 Glasgow University ran the first archaeological excavation of a concrete skatepark here, with skaters of all ages on the trowels. Cheapest parking is the museum car park, about £1 for four hours.

Housed in a former swimming pool in South Bristol, Campus Pool Skatepark is a unique indoor facility that preserves the original pool decor while delivering a modern ride. The park primarily features a concrete street section complete with an 8-foot vert corner, hubbas, and ledges.

For older skaters returning to the board, the standout feature is the dedicated beginners' section and mini ramp. Constructed from high-impact birch ply, this area provides a forgiving surface and physically separates those learning the basics from the heavier traffic of the concrete street course. It is an extremely welcoming environment; just be sure to check their online booking schedule to find the right general session for your schedule.

After moving its operating hub from Nottingham to the Derbion centre in Derby, Flo Skatepark has established itself as a deeply community-focused indoor space. Built entirely of timber, the park features a street plaza, transition sections, and dedicated beginner areas.

The facility explicitly caters to returning and older skaters, running dedicated "Over 30s" sessions and beginner-friendly, coach-led taster sessions that provide a structured, supportive environment for getting comfortable on a board. Advance online booking is highly recommended.

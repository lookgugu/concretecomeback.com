Sheltered under the massive concrete flyover of the M3 between Little Patrick and Corporation streets, Bridges Urban Sports Park gives Belfast an all-weather venue where older skaters do not have to forfeit a session every time it rains. Built and maintained by Belfast City Council, the park stays remarkably dry overhead and is fully floodlit for evening runs after office hours.

The footprint divides cleanly into two worlds: a technical street plaza complete with manny pads, low hubbas, mellow flat banks, and a pyramid, set well back from the towering capsule bowl and oververt pocket. The caveat is that Belfast City Council strictly mandates helmets here, and driving maritime rain can blow damp mist onto the outer edges of the concrete — inspect the surface before dropping in. Corporation Street Car Park sits directly beside the park, with free parking on evenings and weekends.

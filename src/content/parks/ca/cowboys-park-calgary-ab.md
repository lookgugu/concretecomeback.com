The scale is the thing: with this much concrete the sections genuinely are separate, so you can spend a whole session in the beginner area — shallow bowl, bathtub bowl, a four-set with a hubba and rail — without anyone hurrying you towards the clover bowl or the full pipe. Lights keep it usable after dark and the skatepark itself never closes.

Two things to plan around. The park was renamed from Shaw Millennium Park to Cowboys Park and it doubles as Calgary's main festival ground, so from mid-July parts of the park close around events; the city's page lists what is open week to week. Parking is the pay lot beside Mewata off 11th Street, then a short roll across the park past the fountain.

Located adjacent to the Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre, this New Line-designed facility solves the biggest problem with skating in the Pacific Northwest: the rain. 

The park's centerpiece is a large, architecturally striking roof that completely covers the deep concrete bowl and adjacent transition elements, keeping them perfectly dry during wet weather. Combined with high-quality floodlights and a 24-hour operating policy, it is one of the most reliable options in the Lower Mainland for older skaters who need to squeeze in a session around work hours, regardless of the forecast.

CJ's is a staple for year-round skating in the Greater Toronto Area. As a massive indoor facility, it's primarily built from wood and offers a wide variety of terrain, from mellow street sections and mini-ramps to larger transitions. 

For older returning skaters, the real draw is their programming. They host an "Adult Skateboarders Group Lesson" on Saturday mornings that explicitly caters to older beginners, providing 45 minutes of coaching followed by free skate time in a highly supportive environment. The park operates on a session basis, so check their schedule and book online before heading over.

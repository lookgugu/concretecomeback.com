Vic West Skatepark is a concrete staple in Victoria, located just across the Johnson Street Bridge from downtown. It balances a variety of street obstacles with transition elements, including a mellow 7-foot capsule bowl that locals cite as one of the best places to learn how to drop in.

For older skaters, Vic West's 24-hour accessibility is its greatest asset. The park is highly regarded as a welcoming locals' hangout, and visiting before 10:00 AM on weekends or before 1:00 PM on weekdays almost guarantees a quiet, relaxed environment. If you are feeling intimidated, local coaching services run sessions here specifically to help adult beginners build confidence in a controlled way.

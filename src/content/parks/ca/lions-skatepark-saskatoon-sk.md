Set within Victoria Park on the west bank of the South Saskatchewan River, the Lions SkatePark is Saskatoon's premier wheeled-sports venue. Designed by New Line Skateparks and spanning 20,000 square feet, the park provides an open-concept concrete layout that serves everyone from first-timers to seasoned veterans.

The facility's layout works especially well for older skaters because the street course is cleanly divided from the deep transition bowl. A wide flatground plaza equipped with knee-high ledges, low flat-rails, and gradual roll-in banks offers an unintimidating space to practice pushing, turning, and low-consequence grind variations without dodging high-speed bowl lines. When you want to warm up on transitions, the mellow perimeter quarter pipes provide easy entry angles and comfortable coping.

The park is free, open daily from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM, and benefits from a tranquil riverside setting that stays peaceful during weekday mornings between 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM. There are no floodlights, so riding ends as evening light dims. If you need fresh hardware, bearings, or setup advice, stop by Ninetimes Skateshop on Louise Avenue across the river.

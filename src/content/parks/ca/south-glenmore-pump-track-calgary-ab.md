The South Glenmore Bicycle Pump Track is a custom-designed asphalt circuit located within South Glenmore Park. While technically built with bikes in mind, the smooth asphalt rollers and berms make it an excellent low-impact playground for skateboarders working on balance and pumping mechanics.

What makes this track stand out for older skaters is its built-in separation of ability levels. The facility is split into two distinct loops: a forgiving 110-meter track dedicated to beginners, and a longer 204-meter track for advanced momentum. Because it is a highly popular outdoor spot, the key to a quiet, mellow session is utilizing the park's early opening hours (before 9:00 AM) or visiting during weekday school hours to avoid the crowds.

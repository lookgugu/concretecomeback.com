Meadowbank Skatepark is a sprawling, modern concrete facility in Ryde, designed by the renowned team at Convic. Known for its ultra-smooth surfaces, the park balances a competition-style deep bowl and heavy street ledges with plenty of approachable terrain.

For older skaters, Meadowbank offers a smaller bowl with a spine and mellow transitions that are perfect for regaining your flow. The spacious layout includes dedicated mellow areas well-suited for beginners to practice without crossing into the main traffic lines. The park is equipped with lights, allowing for extended evening sessions after work, and local coaching groups frequently use the space for private adult lessons.

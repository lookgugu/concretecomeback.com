Leederville sits beside the YMCA's HQ youth centre in the heart of the suburb, which makes it unusually easy to reach without a car: the train station is 350 metres away, and if you do drive there is an hour's free parking with a ticket next to the park. The two bowls joined by a submarine are the signature, but the mini ramp, quarters and blocks give you plenty of lower-commitment terrain, and the lights and 24-hour access mean you can pick the quiet hours.

The WA skate association calls it smooth and well built; a directory listing notes some metal-framed elements alongside the concrete. For a lesson before you commit to the bowls, Beyond Skate in North Perth runs clinics on its own indoor park and is the natural shop to pair with this one.

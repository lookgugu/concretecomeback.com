Spread across the sporting grounds of Bardon Avenue in Miami on the Gold Coast, Pizzey Park is a landmark facility that balances international competition credentials with approachable grassroots terrain. Following a major multi-million-dollar Convic upgrade, the park now covers more than 4,800 square metres of smooth concrete alongside Australia's largest asphalt pump track.

For older skaters, that 2,100-square-metre pump track is a major asset: its gentle rollers, berms, and sweeping curves offer an ideal, low-impact way to regain balance and pump speed without the impact of jarring flat landings. In the main street course, granite ledges and flat rails scale up incrementally from ankle-high curb boxes to advanced drops, giving returners plenty of low-consequence obstacles to dial in basics. LED floodlights keep the concrete well-lit until 10pm every evening, and the precinct features ample parking, public toilets, and shaded spectator viewing.

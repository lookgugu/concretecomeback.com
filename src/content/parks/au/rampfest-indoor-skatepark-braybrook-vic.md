RampFest is a top-tier indoor facility located in Braybrook, renowned for its high-quality wooden ramps and expansive wooden bowl construction. Providing a weather-proof environment, it is a reliable spot for Melbourne locals to progress regardless of the forecast.

For older skaters, RampFest goes above and beyond by running dedicated adult skateboarding classes. Whether you are an absolute beginner or looking to dust off some old transition skills, these sessions offer coaching from experienced professionals in a completely supportive, peer-only environment. As a private facility, session times vary and a waiver is required, so be sure to book your spot online before heading down.

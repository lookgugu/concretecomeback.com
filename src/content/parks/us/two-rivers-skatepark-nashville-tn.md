Situated within Two Rivers Park in east Nashville adjacent to the Stones River Greenway, Two Rivers Skatepark is the centerpiece of Nashville Metro Parks' wheeled sports facilities. The 25,000-square-foot concrete park combines a comprehensive street course with multiple transition zones, backed by evening floodlights that extend sessions until 11pm.

For older skaters easing back into riding, the layout separates low-impact obstacles from the high-velocity deep bowl. The street section features mellow banked ramps, low curb-height ledges, pyramid roll-ins, and manual pads where you can dial in pushing and turning mechanics in open space. Metro Parks enforces a mandatory helmet rule, and early weekday mornings (between 6am and 10am) provide a peaceful, low-traffic window across the smooth concrete.

Weekend afternoons during summer can draw heavier crowds due to the neighbouring water park, so plan for earlier hours if you prefer open lines. Plenty of free parking and park restrooms are located directly next to the course.

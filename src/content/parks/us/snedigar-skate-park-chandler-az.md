Set inside the 90-acre Snedigar Sportsplex in South Chandler, Snedigar Skate Park (also known as Chandler Skate Park) is a 35,000-square-foot poured-concrete facility designed by SITE Design Group and operated by the City of Chandler. For older skaters in the Phoenix metro's East Valley, the park offers three major advantages: early morning openings and full overhead floodlighting until 10:30pm to beat the Sonoran Desert heat, overhead shade screens and an adjacent ramada for sun relief, and a facility policy restricting the concrete to skateboarders and inline skaters only.

The sunken, open-sightline layout makes it easy to ease back into riding without crossing high-speed bowl lines. Immediately inside the front gate, a wide perimeter deck descends down a variety of gentle, low-angle banks and warmup transitions into an expansive street course stocked with blocks, ledges, flatground corridors, and two funboxes (one standard height with a long flat rail and one taller box), while two distinct concrete bowls sit in their own dedicated zone. Keep a few City of Chandler operational caveats in mind: the skatepark is an unsupervised "skate at your own risk" facility, closes for routine maintenance on Wednesday mornings until 2:30pm (and until 3:00pm on the last Wednesday of each month), enforces a strict nightly lights-out closing signaled by a digital clock, and locks its gates entirely whenever rain leaves standing water on the concrete. Before heading down Loop 101 or I-10, stop by [Cowtown Skateboards](/directory/shops/us/cowtown-skateboards-phoenix-az) in Phoenix for softer wheels or pads, or pair your trip with nearby [Pecos Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/pecos-skatepark-phoenix-az) in Ahwatukee and [Rio Vista Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/rio-vista-skatepark-peoria-az) in Peoria.

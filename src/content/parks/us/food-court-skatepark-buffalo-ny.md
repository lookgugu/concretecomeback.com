Food Court Skatepark is a unique 15,000 square foot indoor facility located inside the McKinley Mall in Buffalo. The park features a wood surface covered with Traxion material, guaranteeing a smooth and grippy ride that won't weather over time.

Its controlled indoor setting and spacious flat ground make it a highly approachable space for older skaters rebuilding their confidence away from the outdoor elements. The park heavily encourages progression by offering scheduled lessons by appointment. Note that the facility closes for an hour daily between 4:00 PM and 5:00 PM for cleaning and maintenance.

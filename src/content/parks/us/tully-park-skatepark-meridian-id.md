Set inside 17-acre Tully Park along North Linder Road in the Boise metro's Treasure Valley, Tully Park Skatepark is a 15,000-square-foot poured-concrete facility designed and built by Evergreen Skateparks. Instead of a cramped, stair-heavy plaza, Evergreen shaped the park as a continuous "skate landscape" where older skaters can build leg strength, balance, and carving rhythm through low-impact pumping without needing to pop high-impact tricks.

The layout loops a long oval flow track around a central grass island, stringing together gentle rolling humps, hips, and hand-painted mural banks trimmed in blue and purple coping, with low street ledges, a flat rail, and a bank-to-rail folded smoothly into the perimeter run. Once you are warmed up on the outer loop, you can step into the inner moonscape cluster of interconnected pockets and shallow bowls. Off-street parking, restrooms, a drinking fountain, and shaded picnic shelters sit immediately adjacent inside Tully Park. Note that unlike [Rhodes Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/rhodes-skatepark-boise-id) in downtown Boise, Tully Park Skatepark has no dedicated night floodlighting and closes 30 minutes after sunset, and BMX bikes share the concrete—aim for weekday mornings for the calmest flow laps, and swing by [Prestige Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/prestige-skateshop-boise-id) in Boise if you need pads or hardware.

Located at 605 Hillcrest Loop beside the Robert E. Russell Sports Complex just east of Hattiesburg, Petal Skateboard Park (also known as Petal Skatepark) is a 14,000-square-foot poured-concrete municipal facility designed and built by California Skateparks. Open and floodlit daily from 7:00am to 10:00pm with free on-site complex parking, it gives older skaters in South Mississippi and the Pine Belt a reliable venue for early-morning warmup sessions and cooler evening skates after work.

California Skateparks laid out the 14,000-square-foot slab to separate street and beginner transition from deeper vertical terrain: returning riders can start on the wide flatground approaches, mellow bank ramps, low grind ledges, manual pads, Euro gap, hubba ledges, and handrail in the street section, then step into the smaller fun bowl with a transfer spine before attempting the deeper pool-style bowl. Keep a few operational caveats in mind: the facility is unsupervised ("skate at your own risk") and shared with BMX riders and scooters, helmets and pads are strongly recommended, and summer afternoon heat in southern Mississippi makes the 7:00am opening window or lit evening hours up to 10:00pm the most comfortable times to ride. Skaters travelling north through Mississippi can pair a Pine Belt session here with [Oxford Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/oxford-skate-park-ms) in Oxford or the user-activated evening lights at [Tupelo Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/tupelo-skatepark-ms) in Tupelo.

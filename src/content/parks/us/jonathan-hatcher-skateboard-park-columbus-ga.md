Set near the Chattahoochee Riverfront at 200 4th Street and Victory Drive, the Jonathan Hatcher Skateboard Park is a 32,000-square-foot poured-concrete facility built by California Skateparks and operated by Columbus Parks and Recreation (Columbus Consolidated Government). Open daily from 5:00am to 11:00pm with overhead night lighting and free on-site parking, the massive complex gives older skaters in West Georgia and the Chattahoochee Valley expansive dawn quiet windows, shaded river breezes, and long evening sessions after work—supported by a municipal rule requiring parent or guardian supervision for all users under age 12.

California Skateparks divided the 32,000-square-foot footprint into distinct street, flow, and pool zones so returning riders can progress without crossing into heavy vertical lines: you can start in the street plaza (seven ledges, two angled rails, banks, a pyramid, a small 4-stair, a larger 6-stair, and a two-step pocket "love seat"), then carve through two connected flow bowls featuring speed humps, bank walls, two smaller spine-linked bowls, and a racetrack-style flow section (6 to 9 feet) before eyeing the advanced Peanut Bowl finished with real pool tile, concrete coping, and dead boxes. Keep strict Columbus Consolidated Government rules in mind before visiting: securely strapped helmets, knee pads, elbow pads, and wrist guards are mandatory for all riders; metal bike pegs and waxing rails are prohibited; and the 11:00pm closing curfew is strictly enforced under criminal trespassing and equipment-seizure ordinances. Skaters heading north up I-185 toward Metro Atlanta can pair a session here with [Historic Fourth Ward Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/historic-fourth-ward-skatepark-atlanta-ga) in Atlanta, [Brook Run Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/brook-run-skate-park-dunwoody-ga) in Dunwoody, or [Kennesaw Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/kennesaw-skatepark-ga) in Kennesaw.

All Together Skatepark is an indoor staple in Seattle that provides a dry, welcoming environment when the weather turns. It’s located underneath the evo store and features smooth Skatelite surfaces over a mix of street obstacles and a mini-ramp. The park is highly regarded for its inclusive vibe and regularly scheduled programming.

Older skaters will appreciate the weekly "30+ OG Night" on Sundays from 7–10 PM, creating a comfortable space to practice without the usual crowds. They also offer adult skate camp programs for those looking to rebuild their confidence on a board. Remember to check their online schedule before visiting, as session times vary throughout the week. Pair your visit with a stop at local shop 35th North.

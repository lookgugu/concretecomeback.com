Situated inside Hillside Lions Park along East Boulevard Avenue and North 17th Street, Lions Park Skate Park is Bismarck's central municipal concrete skate facility, maintained by the Bismarck Parks and Recreation District (BPRD). Open daily from 6:00am to 11:00pm with adjacent off-street parking, picnic shelters, walking trails, and seasonal park restrooms, the venue gives older skaters in North Dakota's capital city reliable early-morning quiet windows before afternoon school crowds arrive.

The multi-level poured-concrete course separates open street and transition warmup zones from its deeper features: returning riders can start on the flatground approaches, multiple grind boxes with coping rails, two stair sets, two hubba walls, and gentle level-to-level bank transitions before dropping into the concrete half-bowl and half-pipe section. Keep BPRD operational rules and caveats in mind: the park is an unsupervised multi-wheel facility shared by skateboards, inline skates, and bicycles, helmets and pads are strongly recommended, glass beverage containers and motorized vehicles are prohibited, and smoking is barred within 20 feet of park structures. Skaters in Bismarck can warm up on the smaller neighborhood short half-pipe at [Sons of Norway Park Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/sons-of-norway-park-skate-park-bismarck-nd) on the south side of town, or cross North Dakota along I-94 to [Dike West Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/dike-west-skatepark-fargo-nd) in Fargo and north on I-29 to [Rydell Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/rydell-skatepark-grand-forks-nd) in Grand Forks.

As the largest public skatepark in San Antonio, Pearsall Park spreads its 15,000 square feet across a wide footprint, physically separating the deep bowls from the more mellow flatground and street lines. The park is exceptionally friendly for older skaters thanks to its long hours—opening at 5:00 AM and shutting down the lights at 11:00 PM—offering ample opportunity to dodge both the crowds and the midday Texas sun.

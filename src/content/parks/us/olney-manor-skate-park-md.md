Set inside the 61-acre Olney Manor Recreational Park along Georgia Avenue in northern Montgomery County, Olney Manor Skate Park is a 14,400-square-foot (90-by-160-foot) poured-concrete facility operated by Montgomery Parks. Open daily from sunrise to 10:00pm with free admission and evening lighting, the park gives older skaters in the Maryland suburbs north of Washington DC and west of Baltimore a reliable spot for dawn warmups and weeknight sessions—supported by Montgomery Parks rules that restrict the enclosure to skateboarders and inline skaters (excluding bicycles) and require a parent or guardian to accompany any skater age 8 or younger.

The rectangular 90-by-160-foot slab cleanly divides its street and transition obstacles from its deep bowl terrain. Returning skaters can start along the perimeter quarter-pipes, manual pad, pyramid ledge, and funbox with a flat rail before working toward the stair set with handrail and hubba ledge or dropping into the 10-foot-deep clover bowl at the end of the park. Keep Montgomery Parks operational caveats in mind: the facility is unsupervised, and park staff lock the gates whenever standing water, rain, snow, or ice create slick surface conditions. For additional concrete sessions across Maryland and the Capital Region, pair a visit here with [Skatepark of Baltimore](/directory/parks/us/skatepark-of-baltimore-md) and [Hazelwood Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/hazelwood-skatepark-baltimore-md) in Baltimore or [Green Skate Lab](/directory/parks/us/green-skate-lab-langdon-park-washington-dc) in northeast Washington DC.

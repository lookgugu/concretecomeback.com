Set in the foothills of southern Henderson inside the 53-acre Anthem Hills Park & Anthem East Trailhead complex at Reunion Drive and Sonatina Drive, Anthem Hills Skate Park is a 26,500-square-foot poured-concrete facility designed by SITE Design Group and operated by the City of Henderson. Open daily from 7:00am to 10:00pm with full evening floodlighting, shaded picnic shelters, on-site restrooms, and an adjacent outdoor roller hockey rink, the park gives older skaters in the southeast Las Vegas Valley reliable early-morning and post-sunset windows to escape Mojave Desert heat—helped by a City of Henderson rule requiring a parent or guardian to accompany any park user under the age of 13.

SITE Design Group arranged the 26,500-square-foot footprint as a progressive linear flow course that steps up in depth as you move deeper into the park. Returning skaters can start near the entrance on the street section—equipped with a hubba ledge, flat rail, bank-to-wall, and a funbox with a flat bar and transfer ledge—before carving around the corner into a long, roller-coaster-style snake run built with forgiving rolled lips, elevation changes, rollers, a spine, and two rolled volcanoes. At the far end, the run hooks into an 8-foot-deep square bowl with a hip alongside a separate kidney pool featuring a 5-foot shallow end and an 8-foot deep end. Keep City of Henderson rules in mind: helmets, elbow pads, knee pads, shirts, and shoes are mandatory for all riders; no food or drinks other than water are allowed inside the skatepark; BMX bikes share the concrete; and the park closes whenever surfaces are wet or covered in desert debris. Pair your southern Nevada trip with [Desert Breeze Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/desert-breeze-skate-park-las-vegas-nv) in Las Vegas or [Craig Ranch Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/craig-ranch-skatepark-north-las-vegas-nv) in North Las Vegas.

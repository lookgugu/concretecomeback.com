Switch and Signal is a massive 12,000-square-foot indoor skatepark built inside a former bowling alley. The ramps are surfaced with Russian birch wood, providing a smooth and forgiving ride. 

For older skaters, the highlight is the dedicated Adult Beginner Session held on the first Monday of each month from 7:00 pm to 9:30 pm. This specialized block guarantees a slower-paced, supportive environment to learn the basics without the intimidation of a crowded park. They also offer a full calendar of structured classes ("Learn to Skateboard") and free board and pad rentals for newcomers. The facility includes a fully stocked [on-site skate shop](/directory/shops/us/switch-and-signal-skateshop-swissvale-pa) with everything you need to get rolling. Booking sessions in advance online is highly recommended.

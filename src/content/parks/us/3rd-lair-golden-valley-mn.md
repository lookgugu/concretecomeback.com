A staple of the Twin Cities scene, 3rd Lair features a massive indoor wooden skatepark tailored to riders of all abilities. The layout offers plenty of varied transitions, a bowl, and an approachable street section.

For older skaters, the park stands out by offering structured adult skateboard clinics and designated 30+ nights with discounted admission. These dedicated times provide a supportive, low-pressure atmosphere to practice fundamentals away from the standard crowded sessions.

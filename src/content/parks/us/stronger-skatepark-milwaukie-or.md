Located just outside Portland in Milwaukie, Stronger Skatepark is purpose-built to be an inclusive, low-intimidation space for all ages. The park features a Baltic birch top layer over its wood ramps and focuses heavily on flatground, mellow street obstacles, and transition learning areas.

It is a standout facility for older skaters returning to the sport. They offer an adult skateboarding class every Monday night, as well as specific 30+ sessions bookable on their calendar. Weekday afternoons before 5 PM are generally quieter if you want to work on basics. Check out nearby Cal Skate Skateboards when you're in the Portland area.

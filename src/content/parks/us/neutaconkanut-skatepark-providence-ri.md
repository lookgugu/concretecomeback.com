Set within the expansive Neutaconkanut Park and Hill recreation area, this concrete skatepark is one of the oldest and largest in Providence. Despite its age, it boasts smooth, well-maintained surfaces that encourage creative, flowing lines rather than high-impact stunts.

Older skaters will appreciate the approachable small bowl and pump track, which offer excellent, low-risk spaces for dialing in your balance and rhythm. The park operates from sunrise to sunset and is surrounded by extensive walking trails and green spaces. Morning visits are recommended to enjoy the park at its quietest.

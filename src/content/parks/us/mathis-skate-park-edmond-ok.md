Set in the northeast section of 280-acre Mitch Park along West Covell Road, Mathis Skate Park (also known locally as Mathis Brothers Skate Park) is a 17,500-square-foot poured-concrete facility operated by the City of Edmond Parks and Recreation Department. Open daily from 7:00am to 11:00pm with overhead night lighting running seven days a week, the half-acre complex gives older skaters in the northern Oklahoma City metro both quiet early-morning sessions and illuminated evening runs after work, backed by adjacent restrooms, a drinking fountain, and paved multi-use trails.

The concrete footprint spreads across multiple bays of transition and street terrain, allowing returning skaters to warm up on outer flatground aprons, gentle banks, low ledges, and mellow quarter-pipes before stepping up to the larger half-pipe walls and bowl pockets. Keep a few operational caveats in mind: Mathis Skate Park is an unsupervised all-wheel municipal facility shared with inline skaters and BMX riders, and nearby Mitch Park athletic complexes, amphitheater events, and the Edmond YMCA draw heavy family and youth crowds on weekend afternoons—making weekday mornings after 7:00am or lit evening sessions after 8:00pm the calmest times to ride. Skaters exploring central Oklahoma can pair an Edmond session with [Mat Hoffman Action Sports Park](/directory/parks/us/mat-hoffman-action-sports-park-okc) in downtown Oklahoma City or head up the Turner Turnpike to [Gathering Place Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/gathering-place-skate-park-tulsa-ok) in Tulsa.

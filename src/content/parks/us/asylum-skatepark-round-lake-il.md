Asylum Skatepark is a massive indoor facility serving the greater Chicago area, now located in Round Lake. Built entirely of wood, the park provides a forgiving surface and includes everything from a kidney bowl with extensions to comprehensive street sections and mini-ramps. 

For older skaters, the highlight is the "Old Man Skate Jam" held on Wednesday nights specifically for ages 30+. This session provides a relaxed environment to skate the bowl or practice street lines without navigating the typical weekend crowds. Helmets are always a good idea, and the park also runs beginner clinics on weekend mornings if you need a structured refresher. Stop by Uprise in Chicago for gear needs.

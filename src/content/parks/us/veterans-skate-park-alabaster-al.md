Set inside 90-acre Veterans Park along Highway 119 in Shelby County just south of Birmingham, Veterans Skate Park is a 7,500-square-foot poured-concrete facility designed by SITE Design Group and built by California Skateparks for the City of Alabaster. For older skaters in the southern Birmingham metro, its standout draw is a strict municipal rule: bicycles and scooters are explicitly prohibited on the skatepark slab, reserving the concrete for skateboards and inline skates and eliminating unpredictable scooter cross-traffic.

The layout separates a street-style plaza—equipped with open flatground approaches, manual pads, low ledges, a 3-stair with a hubba ledge, and 5-stair and 8-stair handrails—from its signature rounded "skull bowl," which ranges from 6 to 10 feet deep with real pool tile and pool coping. Keep City of Alabaster rules and site caveats in mind before heading out: management strictly requires a helmet with a fastened chin strap at all times (with knee pads, elbow pads, and wrist guards strongly recommended), and while the surrounding park's walking trails are lit until 11:00pm, the skatepark itself has no dedicated overhead sports floodlighting and is best skated during daylight hours. If you want a covered, floodlit session in downtown Birmingham, pair a south-metro ride here with [City Walk Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/city-walk-skatepark-birmingham-al) or head north up I-65 to [Get-A-Way Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/get-a-way-skatepark-huntsville-al) in Huntsville.

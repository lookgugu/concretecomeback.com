Operated by the Watertown Parks and Recreation Department along 21st Street SW, Watertown Skatepark is a 12,000-square-foot all-concrete skatepark and pump-track hybrid designed and built by Evergreen Skateparks. Explicitly engineered to accommodate all ability levels, the poured-concrete course combines a 4-foot mini-ramp that flows into a bank with a tabletop and quarter-pipes, seven 4-to-5-foot quarter-pipes, a 2.5-foot launch tabletop, three approachable 18-to-30-inch pyramids (two topped with grind boxes), banks, and rails. For older skaters getting back on a board, the low pyramids, 4-foot mini-ramp, and pump-track transitions allow continuous low-impact carving without requiring high-consequence drops.

Watertown Parks and Recreation enforces structured operating rules that keep sessions orderly: scooters are prohibited on the slab, and bicycle riders are separated into dedicated "BMX Only" windows on Wednesdays from 10:00am to 1:00pm and 6:00pm to 8:00pm. Every skater must have a signed waiver on file and wear a helmet to enter (no one is admitted without both, and wrist supports, knee pads, and elbow pads are strongly recommended). Note the seasonal schedule and caveats before travelling: the park is unlit and closes for the winter season on October 10th until spring weather permits reopening, and skating is prohibited whenever the concrete is wet or icy. Skaters driving the I-29 corridor in eastern South Dakota can pair a session in Watertown with [Dwiggins Medary Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/dwiggins-medary-skatepark-brookings-sd) in Brookings or [Barb Iverson Skate Plaza](/directory/parks/us/barb-iverson-skate-plaza-sioux-falls-sd) in Sioux Falls.

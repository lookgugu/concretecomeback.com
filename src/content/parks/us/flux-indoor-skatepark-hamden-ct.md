FLUX is far more than a traditional skatepark—it is a combined action sports and wellness hub. The indoor wooden park features a layout with large down ledges and round coping, designed with significant input from the local community to create a smooth, accessible flow.

What truly sets FLUX apart for older skaters is its holistic approach to the sport. The facility includes a dedicated recovery lounge equipped with infrared saunas, compression boots, and massage tools, alongside a standard gym. While public hours run Thursday through Sunday, members receive a key code for 7-day access, allowing for completely flexible scheduling away from peak crowds.

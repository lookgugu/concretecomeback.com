Woodward Park City’s indoor hub is built entirely around the philosophy of safe progression, making it a goldmine for returning skaters. Its strongest draw for older skaters is the weekly Tuesday Adult Night (18+), giving you a dedicated two-hour window to access the entire facility among peers rather than teenagers.

The massive indoor layout combines a polished concrete skatepark with wood ramp zones, pump tracks, foam pits, and resi-landings—allowing you to dial in new tricks with a soft landing. Because this is a premium facility, sessions must be booked online and a waiver is required.

Orlando Skate Park provides a welcoming 15,000-square-foot outdoor environment located centrally in the Milk District. For older skaters returning to the sport, the park's explicitly designated beginner’s area offers a safe, physically separated space to warm up away from the deep bowls and advanced flow lines. Extensive lighting allows for cooler evening sessions out of the harsh Florida sun.

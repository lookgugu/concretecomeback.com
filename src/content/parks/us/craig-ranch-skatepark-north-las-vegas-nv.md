Craig Ranch Skatepark is an expansive, 65,000-square-foot concrete facility built into the terrain of Craig Ranch Regional Park. As one of the largest skateparks in the state, it features a diverse array of transition and street elements, from deep bowls and vert sections to ledges and flowy street lines. 

For older skaters, the biggest draw is the schedule and the climate control strategy. The park opens at 7:00 AM daily, making the early morning the perfect window for a quiet, mellow session before the crowds arrive and the desert sun heats up the concrete. Alternatively, the park is fully equipped with lights for evening sessions.

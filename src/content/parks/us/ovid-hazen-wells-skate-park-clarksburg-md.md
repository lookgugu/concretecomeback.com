Ovid Hazen Wells Skate Park opened in March 2026 as the fourth Montgomery Parks skatepark and the first in the northern part of the county. The 12,000-square-foot concrete park was designed by Spohn Ranch after Montgomery Parks staff met with hundreds of local skateboarders. It sits inside the 290-acre Ovid Hazen Wells Recreational Park in Clarksburg, which reopened in May 2026 with a restored carousel, playgrounds, picnic shelters and trails.

For older skaters finding their feet again, the draw is the bowl. Montgomery Parks calls the tiered, peanut-shaped bowl mellow, and its opening announcement notes a roll-in, so you can enter without dropping in. The street section offers quarter pipes and radius wedges, a bank to a brick-pattern slappy curb, a flat bar, a rainbow rail and a kinked round rail. A stair set with handrail and grind ledge, a volcano and a Euro gap are there for later. Start with the shallow end of the bowl and the slappy curb.

Plan around three things. The park follows recreational park hours of sunrise to sunset, so winter after-work sessions are short. It is open to scooters and bikes as well as skateboards and inline skates, and local skaters report that it fills up quickly at busy times, so early mornings are the calmer window. The skatepark is also a walk from the parking lots. Restrooms are at the carousel house from April to October, with portable toilets in the park.

For lit evening sessions in Montgomery County, pair this park with [Olney Manor Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/olney-manor-skate-park-md), open until 10pm, or the supervised [Gaithersburg Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/gaithersburg-skate-park-md). [Blandair Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/blandair-skatepark-columbia-md) in Columbia is a lighted option to the east.

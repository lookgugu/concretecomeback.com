The largest skatepark in the Southwest is a massive concrete facility that caters to all skill levels. While its deep bowls attract advanced riders, the park is highly regarded by older skaters for its thoughtful beginner-friendly zones. A large drop-in area with gentle ramps and a small center bump provides a safe, low-stakes environment to practice rolling and basic transitions.

Adding to its appeal is the recently installed pump track located near the parking lot—a perfect spot to warm up or practice flow without the pressure of the main park. The atmosphere is generally described as chill and welcoming, with night lighting extending the session hours. Helmet use is mandatory for those under 18 and strongly recommended for adults.

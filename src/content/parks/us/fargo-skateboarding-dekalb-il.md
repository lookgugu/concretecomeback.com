Fargo Skateboarding is a nearly 10,000-square-foot heated indoor facility in DeKalb, constructed primarily with smooth wooden ramps. It is designed to be an accessible, community-focused space that caters directly to progression.

Older skaters are exceptionally well-supported here. The facility hosts specific beginner sessions (typically on Saturdays) that provide a low-stress environment supervised by coaches. Beyond the beginner blocks, they offer private group classes structured specifically for adults. The building also houses an [on-site skate shop](/directory/shops/us/fargo-skateboarding-shop-dekalb-il) that can provide all the necessary protective gear and completes to get you started safely. Call ahead to confirm the schedule for the next adult-specific session.

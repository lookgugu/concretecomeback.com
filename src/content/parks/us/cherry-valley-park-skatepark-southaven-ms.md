Located directly south of the football complex parking lot inside Cherry Valley Park at 308 Harmony Road in Southaven (DeSoto County, just across the Tennessee state line from Memphis), the Cherry Valley Park Skatepark and Pump Track is an approximate $1 million municipal facility developed by the City of Southaven and built by American Ramp Company in 2025. Set alongside renovated walking trails, perimeter fencing, and off-street parking, it gives older skaters in northern Mississippi and the greater Memphis metro a modern multi-zone facility away from downtown traffic.

The layout separates two distinct riding surfaces so returning older skaters can ease into a session without immediately dropping into transition or dodging flip-trick traffic. An adjacent sloped and banked asphalt pump track provides a continuous roller-and-berm loop where riders can build leg stamina, balance, and pumping speed without pushing, while the cast-in-place concrete skatepark combines an open street plaza of mellow banks, ledges, and flat rails with a concrete bowl. Keep several municipal rules and caveats in mind: to protect the facility, the City of Southaven closes and locks the park gates at sunset, so sessions are strictly limited to daylight hours; the complex is monitored by security cameras and periodic parks personnel checks; and the pump track and street plaza are shared with BMX bikes and scooters during weekend and after-school peaks. For sessions across north Mississippi or just across the border in Memphis, pair a visit here with [Society Skatepark & Coffee](/directory/parks/us/society-memphis-tn) (and its on-site pro shop [Society Skate Shop](/directory/shops/us/society-memphis-tn)) or [Raleigh Springs Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/raleigh-springs-skate-park-memphis-tn) in Memphis, or head south down I-55 and US-278 to [Oxford Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/oxford-skate-park-ms), [Tupelo Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/tupelo-skatepark-ms), and [Petal Skateboard Park](/directory/parks/us/petal-skateboard-park-ms).

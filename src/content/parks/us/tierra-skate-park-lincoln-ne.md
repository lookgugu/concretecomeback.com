Tucked into 123-acre Tierra/Briarhurst Park near South 27th Street and Highway 2 in south Lincoln, Tierra Skate Park is a free municipal neighborhood facility operated by the Lincoln Parks and Recreation Department. Serving as south Lincoln's primary open outdoor skatepark during citywide park renovations, the layout pairs concrete pump-track rollers and flow transitions with a 4-foot spine, a wide bank, quarter-pipe transitions, a launch box, two rails (one flat rail and one double-kinked round rail), and two progression ledges (one small, one large) running alongside the fun boxes. That side-by-side small-and-large obstacle setup gives older skaters an approachable place to dial back basic grinds, bank turns, and roller pumping without the crowds of a downtown plaza.

Tierra/Briarhurst Park surrounds the skate area with off-street parking, public restrooms, paved multi-use trails, and a KOMPAN FitBLOCK outdoor bodyweight fitness gym right next door that makes pre-session warmups and stretching easy. Keep Lincoln Parks and Recreation operational rules in mind: although the broader park grounds stay open daily from 5:00am to 11:00pm, the skatepark itself has no night lighting and is restricted to daylight hours only. The facility is unsupervised, so protective gear is recommended and weekday mornings offer the quietest sessions before neighborhood school dismissal. Lincoln skaters can pair a daylight outdoor roll here with the dedicated Sunday 19+ indoor evening sessions at [The Bay Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/the-bay-skatepark-lincoln-ne) downtown or head west on I-80 to [Pier Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/pier-park-skatepark-grand-island-ne) in Grand Island.

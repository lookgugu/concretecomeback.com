Riley Skate Park is one of Southeast Michigan's premier skating hotspots, located within Founders Sports Park. Designed by Site Design Group and Grindline, this massive 29,000-square-foot all-concrete park features smooth, flowing terrain with a diverse mix of street and transition obstacles.

What makes it great for older skaters is its vast, spaced-out layout that physically separates different riding zones. This means you can comfortably stick to the mellower areas while advanced traffic flows elsewhere. The park is equipped with lights for evening sessions, making it practical for those skating after work. Proper safety gear, including a helmet and pads, is highly recommended.

Occupying the city block along South Race Street between 2nd and 4th Streets (across from Civic Field) inside Erickson Playfield, the Erickson Skate Park and adjacent Port Angeles Pump Track form the premier public wheel-sports complex on Washington's Olympic Peninsula. Operated by the City of Port Angeles Parks and Recreation Department, the site pairs a 10,000-square-foot poured-concrete skatepark with a modern Velosolutions paved asphalt pump track, accompanied by on-site restrooms, off-street parking, and shaded seating.

Having both surfaces side by side gives returning older skaters a natural, low-impact progression path. You can start on the Velosolutions asphalt pump track—which includes a smaller, gentler ADA-accessible and beginner loop alongside the main roller-and-berm circuit—to warm up your legs and practice pumping speed without pushing or dropping in. From there, step over to the 10,000-square-foot concrete skatepark to work along the perimeter banks, ledges, and rails before carving the flowing concrete bowl. Keep several City of Port Angeles operational caveats in mind: both the skatepark and pump track operate daily from 8:00am until dusk without dedicated overhead skatepark floodlights (though nearby tennis courts have evening lights); the facilities are unsupervised and shared with BMX bikes, scooters, and inline skates, making weekday mornings the calmest window; and coastal Olympic Peninsula rain frequently leaves the concrete and asphalt slick during autumn and winter. When heading across the Puget Sound ferry routes or down Highway 101 toward Tacoma and Seattle, pair a Peninsula session with [Alchemy Indoor Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/alchemy-skatepark-tacoma-wa) in Tacoma, or stop by [35th North](/directory/shops/us/35th-north-seattle-wa) and [Jefferson Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/jefferson-park-seattle-wa) in Seattle.

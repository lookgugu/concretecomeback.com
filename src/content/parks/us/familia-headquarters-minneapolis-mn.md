Familia Headquarters (Familia HQ) is the anchor of the Minneapolis skate scene. Featuring a unique high-grip Traxion surface over wood, the indoor park provides a reliable, climate-controlled space that is highly practical for people skating around work and the harsh midwestern winters.

The facility is widely praised for its "no stupid questions" vibe, making it an excellent starting point for older skaters or beginners. Since it is paired directly with Familia Skateshop, you can easily get advice on gear or a board setup from knowledgeable staff right before hitting the ramps. Call ahead or check their social media to confirm public session times.

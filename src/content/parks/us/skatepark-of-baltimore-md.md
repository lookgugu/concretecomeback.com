Located in Hampden's Roosevelt Park, this volunteer-driven public facility features a world-class deep bowl and a sprawling street section with plenty of smooth concrete for carving and flatground practice.

While peak afternoon hours can be hectic, older skaters frequently gather during quiet weekday or early weekend mornings. Additionally, local programs like Discovery Skate offer tailored lessons on-site, providing adult beginners with a structured path to get comfortable on their boards.

Spanning 21,500 square feet within Astoria Park beside the Robert F. Kennedy (Triborough) Bridge approach, Astoria Skatepark is one of NYC's largest and most functional public skate plazas. The open-air setting along the East River waterfront provides cooling breezes and a spacious concrete layout.

The plaza layout features generous flatground runways and low-rise manual pads and ledges completely separated from the multi-stair and rail setups. Gates unlock at 6am daily; the dawn window between 6am and 9am is exceptionally peaceful, giving older skaters room to carve and practice technical fundamentals before school breaks. Pair your session with a visit to Labor Skateshop on Canal Street in the Lower East Side for fresh wheels and hardware.

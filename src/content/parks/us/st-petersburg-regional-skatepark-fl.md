Located at 600 12th Street South at the southern edge of Campbell Park near downtown St. Petersburg and the Pinellas Trail, the St. Petersburg Regional Skatepark is an approximate 22,000-square-foot custom poured-concrete facility designed and built by Team Pain Skate Parks for the City of St. Petersburg Parks and Recreation Department. Open daily from 10:00am to 11:00pm with free admission, overhead evening lighting, on-site parking, and restrooms, it serves as the flagship public concrete skatepark on the Pinellas County side of Tampa Bay.

Team Pain laid out the expansive slab in three distinct sections so older skaters can build momentum at their own pace without wandering into expert vert lines. Returning riders can start across the sprawling street plaza—working on flatground, graduated ledges, manual pads, flat bars, banks, and quarter-pipes—or pump through the long, undulating concrete snake run before stepping up to the separated 12-foot-deep vertical pool/bowl at the far end of the park. Keep several City of St. Petersburg rules and local caveats in mind: posted municipal rules require all skaters to wear a helmet at all times and strongly emphasize full protective gear; police enforce a strict no-trespassing curfew outside the 10:00am–11:00pm operating window; and Florida summer heat and afternoon thunderstorms make morning openings or lit sessions after dark the most comfortable times to ride. Across the Howard Frankland or Gandy bridges, you can pair a Pinellas session here with the indoor wood ramps and annual 30+ Old Man Bowl contest at [Skatepark of Tampa (SPoT)](/directory/parks/us/skatepark-of-tampa-fl) in Tampa, or head east along I-4 to [Orlando Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/orlando-skate-park-fl) in Orlando.

Located at the northeast corner of Kailua District Park along South Kainalu Drive, Kailua Skate Park is a free municipal concrete facility operated by the City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation (DPR) in partnership with the nonprofit Action Sports Hawaii (ASH). Built across two community-backed phases, the park started in 2012 with a flat poured-concrete slab featuring a metal flatbar, skate bench, and grind boxes, and expanded in Summer 2021 with a Phase 2 poured-concrete quarter-pipe, banks, a wave wall, a kicker rail, a flat rail, and an additional bench. Following coordination with the Kailua Neighborhood Board, evening skatepark lights stay on until 8:00pm on weekdays, giving older skaters on Windward Oahu a reliable after-work session window.

Because the layout focuses on flatground space, mellow banks, a rollable wave wall, and low street obstacles rather than deep vertical bowls, it offers a forgiving progression setup for returning riders. City and County of Honolulu DPR skatepark rules prohibit scooters and motorized vehicles on the concrete, helping keep sightlines predictable, though weekday afternoons right after nearby Kailua Intermediate and Kailua High School let out are noticeably busier than mornings or lit evening hours. The facility is unsupervised ("skate at your own risk"), helmets and pads are recommended, and frequent Windward trade-wind showers can make the slab slick quickly (skating on wet surfaces is prohibited). Oahu skaters can pair a Windward session here with [Kamiloiki Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/kamiloiki-skate-park-honolulu-hi) in Hawaii Kai or [Manana Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/manana-skate-park-pearl-city-hi) in Pearl City.

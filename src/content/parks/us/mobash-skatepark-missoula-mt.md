MoBash Skatepark is a smooth, sprawling concrete facility located within McCormick Park. While it features advanced elements like deep bowls and a full cradle, its layout intentionally includes mellow ramps and practice zones that are perfect for those just learning or regaining their balance.

The park serves as a welcoming community hub for skaters of all ages. Note that the facility is unsupervised and city ordinance strictly requires wearing a properly fastened helmet, so come prepared.

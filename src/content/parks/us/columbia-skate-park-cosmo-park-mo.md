Set at the north end of the 533-acre Columbia Cosmopolitan Recreation Area (Cosmo Park) just off Interstate 70 halfway between St. Louis and Kansas City, Columbia Skate Park is a 28,000-square-foot (175-by-160-foot) poured-concrete course operated by Columbia Parks and Recreation, complemented in the northeast corner of the same park by the Cosmo Bike Park's two paved asphalt pump tracks. For older skaters in mid-Missouri, the facility stands out for a deliberate municipal safety policy: after studying collision risks at unsupervised facilities, the City of Columbia restricted the 28,000-square-foot concrete skatepark exclusively to skateboards and inline skates, prohibiting bicycles and scooters on the slab.

Returning riders have several ways to ease into a session across Cosmo Park. You can warm up on the flat surface of the adjacent outdoor roller hockey rink or carve low-impact laps on the two paved asphalt pump tracks at Cosmo Bike Park (which explicitly encourage riders to "start small and build upwards"), then step onto the concrete skatepark's 16-foot lowbox, 150 feet of quarter-pipe, 30 feet of half-pipe, 15-foot taco, 25-by-25-foot pyramid, 30-foot funbox, 9-foot bank, stairs, and 75 feet of grindrail. Note a few City of Columbia caveats before driving out: although electrical conduit was laid during construction, the skatepark itself has no overhead lighting and closes strictly at dusk; the nearby Antimi Sports Complex restrooms operate seasonally from April 15 to October 15 (with a portable toilet placed at the skatepark from October 15 to April 15); and City Ordinance Section 16-238 mandates strapped helmets for riders age 15 and under while strongly recommending protective gear for older skaters. Travelling east or west along I-70? Pair your stop with [Penn Valley Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/penn-valley-skatepark-kansas-city-mo) and [Escapist Skateboarding](/directory/shops/us/escapist-skateboarding-kansas-city-mo) in Kansas City or [Peter Mathews Memorial Skate Garden](/directory/parks/us/peter-mathews-memorial-skate-park-st-louis-mo) in St. Louis.

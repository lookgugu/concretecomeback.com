Located near the corner of North Clinton Street and 4th Street inside historic Lawton Park just north of downtown Fort Wayne, Lawton Skatepark is a 20,000-square-foot poured-concrete facility (built with 500 cubic yards of concrete and 9 tons of reinforcing steel) managed by the Fort Wayne Parks and Recreation Department. For older skaters in northeastern Indiana, Lawton combines three standout features: a strict municipal rule restricting the fenced enclosure to skateboards and inline skates only (prohibiting bicycles and scooters), designated beginner, intermediate, and advanced riding areas, and user-activated push-button floodlights that extend sessions past sunset.

Entering through the single turnstile on the east side of the 8-foot perimeter fence, returning skaters can warm up on the beginner and intermediate street cityscape—stocked with wide flatground approaches, mellow banks, low ledges, manual pads, flat bars, and funboxes—before eyeing the advanced bowl and its steep 9-foot drop-off. Keep a few Fort Wayne Parks operational caveats in mind: after dark you must press the on-site activation button to turn on the pole lights, which automatically shut off at 10:45pm as a 15-minute warning before the 11:00pm park closure, and the park closes seasonally during winter snow and ice. If you are travelling down I-69 toward central Indiana, you can get a full board evaluation at [Minus Skate Shop](/directory/shops/us/minus-skateshop-carmel-in) in Carmel before skating [Major Taylor Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/major-taylor-skatepark-indianapolis-in) or indoor [Q Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/q-skatepark-indianapolis-in) in Indianapolis.

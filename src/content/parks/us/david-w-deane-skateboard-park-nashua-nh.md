Set off Stadium Drive near Stellos Stadium and Conway Arena in western Nashua, the David W. Deane Skateboard Park is a roughly 20,000-square-foot poured-concrete municipal facility designed by Pillar Design Studios and operated by the City of Nashua Parks and Recreation Department. Open daily from 8:00am to 10:00pm with overhead facility lighting and adjacent stadium-lot parking, the park gives older skaters in southern New Hampshire and the Merrimack Valley both calm weekday morning sessions after 8:00am and dependable lit evening windows after work.

The expansive concrete footprint separates a wide street plaza—featuring smooth flatground approaches, mellow bank ramps, low ledges, manual pads, flat bars, and stair sets—from its transition bowls and quarter-pipes, while City of Nashua rules prohibit bicycles with any form of pegs and require parental supervision for children under age 11. Plan around several strict municipal regulations before visiting: the park is unsupervised ("use at your own risk"), helmets, knee and elbow pads, and wrist supports are required by posted city rules, food, beverages, glass containers, alcohol, smoking, vaping, and pets are prohibited inside the fence, and the facility closes whenever rain, snow, or ice leaves the concrete wet. You can also pair a southern New Hampshire session here with [Dover Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/dover-skatepark-nh) or [Chris Rice Skateboard Park](/directory/parks/us/chris-rice-skateboard-park-portsmouth-nh) on the Seacoast.

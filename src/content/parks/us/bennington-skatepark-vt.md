Located at 133 River Street beside the historic downtown railroad station and the Walloomsac Riverwalk in Rotary Park, Bennington Skatepark is a 16,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete facility designed and built by Grindline Skateparks in partnership with the Town of Bennington and local community advocates. Open daily from 7:00am to 9:00pm with overhead evening lighting, park benches, picnic tables, and adjacent municipal parking, it gives older skaters in southern Vermont dependable early-morning and after-work windows without driving north to Chittenden County.

The layout uses Grindline's signature organic flow to separate approachable progression terrain from advanced transition: returning riders can warm up along the outer street plaza's mellow banks, low ledges, flat rails, and a mini-ramp spine section, or carve the classic concrete snake run before testing the deeper square bowl built as a tribute to Bennington's 1990s Cutting Edge skatepark and Dogtown's Dog Bowl. Keep a few operational caveats in mind: the facility is unsupervised ("skate at your own risk") and shared with scooters, inline skates, and small bicycles, protective helmets and pads are strongly recommended, and sessions pause during winter snow or wet weather until the concrete dries. Skaters travelling up Route 7 toward Lake Champlain can pair a southern Vermont session here with the waterfront concrete at [Andy A-Dog Williams Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/andy-a-dog-williams-burlington-vt) in Burlington or the indoor wooden ramps at [Talent Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/talent-skatepark-south-burlington-vt) in South Burlington.

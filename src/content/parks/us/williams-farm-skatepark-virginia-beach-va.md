Located adjacent to the Williams Farm Recreation Center in Virginia Beach, Williams Farm Skatepark is a 25,000-square-foot poured-concrete facility that offers a much more approachable, well-lit alternative to the towering vert and deep bowl at nearby Mount Trashmore. The layout is organized into three distinct zones—an urban street plaza, a central flow park with smooth, roll-in transitions, and a tiered bowl—allowing older skaters to practice flatground turns and gentle bank-to-bank carving in the plaza or flow section without dropping into the deep end.

Overhead LED floodlighting keeps the park rideable into the evening after work hours, while early mornings after sunrise provide the quietest sessions before faster intermediate street skaters, BMX riders, and scooter traffic arrive in the afternoon. Before your first session, note two strict City of Virginia Beach requirements: all participants must register for a free municipal skate park pass, and an industry-standard helmet is mandatory at all times (with knee and elbow pads strongly recommended).

Modern Skate and Surf in Royal Oak is a staple of the Michigan skate scene and a lifesaver during harsh winters. It earns its adult-friendly credentials by regularly hosting 21+ adult-only skate nights and late evening sessions, giving older skaters a relaxed window to ride without dodging a chaotic after-school crowd.

The park is entirely indoor with smooth wooden surfaces, featuring everything from a forgiving mini-ramp section to deep bowls and a varied street course. Be sure to check their website or call ahead for the current week's session schedule and any adult-specific events.

Located inside The Fields at Little Valley sports and recreation complex in southeastern St. George, Little Valley Skate Park is an 8,850-square-foot multi-level poured-concrete facility designed and built by Omega Ramps for the City of St. George Parks and Community Services Department. Equipped with dedicated overhead floodlighting until 10:30pm daily and shade pavilions, the park gives older skaters in Washington County a bright, modern venue for early-morning and late-evening sessions away from southern Utah's triple-digit summer afternoon heat.

Omega Ramps shaped the multi-tier concrete footprint around approachable, confidence-building transition and street obstacles rather than intimidating vertical walls. Older skaters easing back onto a board can start on the long slappy curb, roller, 3-foot and 4-foot quarter-pipes with hips, and the dedicated 4-foot-tall by 18-foot-wide concrete mini-ramp, or work through the street tiers featuring a 45-degree bank, a banked hip, tall and short 14-foot grind boxes, a curved box, a bump-to-bar/ledge, a Euro gap, two round flat bars, and right- and left-hand flat-to-down hubba boxes and rails. Keep a few practical caveats in mind: because the skatepark sits directly beside soccer fields, pickleball courts, and playgrounds inside The Fields at Little Valley, late afternoons and youth tournament evenings draw heavy family, scooter, and youth traffic—making early mornings or the final 90 minutes before the 10:30pm lights-out cutoff the calmest windows. If you are road-tripping along the I-15 corridor, you can pair a St. George session with [Taylorsville Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/taylorsville-skatepark-ut) and [Fairmont Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/fairmont-skatepark-salt-lake-city-ut) in the Salt Lake Valley, or head southwest to [Craig Ranch Regional Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/craig-ranch-skatepark-north-las-vegas-nv) in North Las Vegas.

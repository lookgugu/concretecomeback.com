Dike West is a massive 23,000-square-foot formed concrete facility and the most popular skate park in Fargo. Its sprawling footprint provides a layout that accommodates and separates challenges for beginner, intermediate, and advanced skaters, making it an excellent space for older skaters easing back onto a board.

The park features a smooth riding surface packed with transition elements like a mini ramp with a spine, alongside a 6-to-10-foot bowl. The street section includes an array of banks, ledges, and rails. The concrete can become extremely slippery when wet, so avoid the park during or immediately after rain.

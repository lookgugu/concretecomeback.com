Dike West is a massive 23,000-square-foot formed concrete facility and the most popular skate park in Fargo. Its sprawling footprint provides a layout that accommodates and separates challenges for beginner, intermediate, and advanced skaters, making it an excellent space for older skaters easing back onto a board.

The park features a smooth riding surface packed with transition elements like a mini ramp with a spine, alongside a 6-to-10-foot bowl. The street section includes an array of banks, ledges, and rails. The concrete can become extremely slippery when wet, so avoid the park during or immediately after rain. If you are travelling across North Dakota, you can pair a Fargo session with [Rydell Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/rydell-skatepark-grand-forks-nd) in Grand Forks or head west along I-94 to [Lions Park Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/lions-park-skate-park-bismarck-nd) in Bismarck.

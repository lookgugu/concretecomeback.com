Set inside 10.5-acre Westside Park at the intersection of West 5th Street and Summit Street, Westside Park Skatepark (known locally as the Yankton Skatepark) is a free poured-concrete municipal facility maintained by the City of Yankton Department of Parks and Recreation. Outfitted with tall overhead LED light poles for evening visibility alongside an open-air rest shelter, paved parking, walking paths, and seasonal park restrooms (open May 1 through October 15), it serves as the anchor public skatepark for southeastern South Dakota along the Missouri River.

The layout pairs a concrete bowl and a modified half-pipe/mini-ramp—updated in consultation with local skaters to keep transition heights manageable—with an open street course of quarter-pipes, bank ramps, a central pyramid, low grind ledges, and flat rails, giving older skaters plenty of room to warm up on flatground and low-impact street lines outside the bowl. Keep a few local caveats in mind: the park is an unsupervised facility shared with inline skaters and BMX riders, protective helmets and pads are strongly recommended, and any dogs brought into Westside Park must remain leashed outside the skating surface. If you are travelling north toward the I-29 corridor, you can pair a Missouri River session here with [Barb Iverson Skate Plaza](/directory/parks/us/barb-iverson-skate-plaza-sioux-falls-sd) in Sioux Falls or [Dwiggins-Medary Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/dwiggins-medary-skatepark-brookings-sd) in Brookings.

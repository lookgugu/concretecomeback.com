Located across from the Linden Shelter along 6th Street in Brookside Park, the Georgie Tsushima Memorial Skate Park is a 10,425-square-foot poured-concrete facility maintained by the City of Ames Parks and Recreation Department. Originally opened in 2002 after five years of local student and community fundraising and formally dedicated in October 2016 in memory of Ames skater Georgie Tsushima, the park stays illuminated until 10:30pm daily. Crucially for older skaters looking for predictable lines without cross-traffic, City of Ames Municipal Code Section 19.14 restricts the slab exclusively to skateboards and inline skates, banning bicycles, scooters, coasters, unicycles, and motorized devices under penalty of municipal citation.

The concrete course is laid out for both beginner and experienced skaters, pairing a standalone mini-ramp with open flatground approaches, ledges, fun boxes, banks, stairs, and flat and sloped grinding rails. Because the facility is unsupervised, the City of Ames strongly recommends wearing a helmet, wrist guards, and elbow and knee pads, and prohibits skating when the concrete is wet or icy. Food and beverages must remain in the spectator viewing area outside the skating surface, and a free courtesy telephone is located in the adjacent Brookside Park parking lot for emergencies. Skaters driving the I-35 and US-30 corridors in central Iowa can pair an evening session in Ames with [Lauridsen Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/lauridsen-skatepark-des-moines-ia) in Des Moines or [Riverside Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/riverside-skatepark-cedar-rapids-ia) in Cedar Rapids.

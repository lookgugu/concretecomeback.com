Located just north of Point Twenty-Two Boulevard and east of Mesa Fire Station 221 inside the Eastmark Great Park corridor in southeast Mesa, The Deck at Eastmark (widely known as Eastmark Skate Park) is a 20,000-square-foot custom poured-concrete facility designed and built by Spohn Ranch Skateparks. Entered beneath a signature archway lined with colorful skateboard decks, the park is open daily from sunrise to 10:00pm with overhead lighting, shade canopies, spectator seating, and nearby park restrooms—giving older skaters in Arizona's third-largest city a reliable venue for dawn warm-ups or evening sessions once the Sonoran Desert sun drops.

Spohn Ranch engineered the facility around physical ability separation so returning older skaters can build leg stamina and board control away from flip-trick traffic. Flanking the central plaza are two dedicated concrete pump tracks—a smaller, mellow beginner loop with gentle rollers and banked turns, and a larger intermediate-to-advanced track with deeper berms—allowing you to practice continuous pumping without pushing. Between the tracks, a tiered urban street plaza integrates skateable architectural benches, low ledges, walls, banks, quarter-pipes, a volcano, and stair sets with handrails. Keep a few local caveats in mind: the park is an unsupervised all-wheel facility set inside a family-oriented community park, meaning late afternoons and weekends bring heavy scooter and neighborhood youth traffic; and summer afternoon heat in the East Valley is extreme, making early mornings or lit sessions before 10:00pm essential. For setup advice, softer wheels, or protective pads in the Phoenix metro, stop by [Cowtown Skateboards](/directory/shops/us/cowtown-skateboards-phoenix-az) in Phoenix, or pair your East Valley session with the skateboard-only concrete at [Snedigar Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/snedigar-skate-park-chandler-az) in Chandler and [Pecos Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/pecos-skatepark-phoenix-az) in Ahwatukee.

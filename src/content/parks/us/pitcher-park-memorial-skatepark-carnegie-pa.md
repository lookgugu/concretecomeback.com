Pitcher Park Memorial Skatepark is a standout concrete destination in the Pittsburgh area, famously known for its gnarly bowl and iconic full pipe. However, older skaters returning to the sport will appreciate its thoughtful layout, which explicitly separates ability levels to keep traffic manageable.

The park features a dedicated flatground beginner street area situated on an upper deck, complete with ledge room for seating and resting. This section provides a calm space separate from the intermediate street course and the tiered advanced street section, which contains larger hubbas, handrails, and a euro gap. Some locals note that the park lights can occasionally be dim or out in the bowl area, so ride with caution if visiting near dusk.

Situated along the Jordan Creek Greenway at North 6th Street and Sumner Avenue, Jordan Park Skatepark is a 9,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete facility designed by Grindline Skateparks, built by Spohn Ranch Skateparks, and operated by the City of Allentown Parks and Recreation Department. Equipped with overhead lighting, on-site parking, and spectator seating, the park gives older skaters in Pennsylvania's Lehigh Valley a smooth, modern transition-and-street layout developed with direct input from the local skateboarding community.

Grindline and Spohn Ranch shaped the course so newer and returning skaters have a low-pressure place to start: one end of the street section opens into a generous flatground area, and the far side of the course sets aside smaller, approachable obstacles—including a low grind ledge and a mellow quarter-pipe—out of the main flow line. Once your legs are warm, the rest of the park blends banks, a euro gap, curbs, stairs, hubbas, handrails, a wallride, and a roll-in into a concrete bowl with a spine and extensions. Keep a few practical caveats in mind: the park is an unsupervised all-wheel facility shared with BMX bikes, roller skates, and scooters with no mandatory helmet rule posted, and the on-site park restrooms and drinking fountains are winterized and closed from mid-November through spring. If you are heading south down the Northeast Extension toward Philadelphia, you can pair a Lehigh Valley session with [Paine's Park](/directory/parks/us/paines-park-philadelphia-pa) and pick up fresh gear at [Nocturnal Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/nocturnal-skateshop-philadelphia-pa).

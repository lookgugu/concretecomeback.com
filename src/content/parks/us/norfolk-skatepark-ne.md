Located in Veterans Memorial Park, the Norfolk Skatepark is a smooth 14,500-square-foot concrete facility built by Spohn Ranch. It replaced an aging wood-and-metal setup with a modern, flowing design that suits a variety of skill levels.

For older skaters, the standout features are the mellow 4-to-6-foot bean-shaped bowl and the dedicated mini-ramp section, both of which provide lower-impact transition practice. The inclusion of park lighting ensures you can comfortably skate after dusk until the park closes for the night.

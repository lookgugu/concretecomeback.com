Set on the Town of Scituate's central athletic campus behind Gates Middle School and Scituate High School next to Kazlousky Park, Scituate Skatepark is a poured-concrete facility designed by Spohn Ranch and constructed in 2016 by Misiano Skate Parks. Built in an L-shaped footprint wrapping around an outdoor street hockey rink, the park is divided into three distinct zones: a standalone concrete mini-ramp, a low-impact concrete pump track and roller flow section, and a street course with banks, ledges, and rails. That three-part layout gives older skaters and returning riders room to pump through rollers or session the mini-ramp without crossing through the street plaza's main grind lines, and the Town of Scituate Recreation Department also uses the venue for structured beginner skateboarding clinics.

Ample off-street parking is available in the 400-space school and recreation lot, and campus lighting extends across the recreation complex into the evening (the broader park campus operates from 5:45am to 10:00pm). Because the skatepark sits directly on the public middle and high school campus, weekday dismissal hours between 2:30pm and 5:00pm and youth street hockey schedules bring heavy student traffic; mornings during the school year and early weekend hours are much calmer. The concrete is unsupervised, so helmets and protective pads are strongly recommended. South Shore skaters can pair a session in Scituate with [Beals Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/beals-park-skatepark-weymouth-ma) in Weymouth or pick up parts in the city at [Orchard Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/orchard-skateshop-boston-ma) in Boston.

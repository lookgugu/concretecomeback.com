Located in the Stout Field industrial district about seven minutes southwest of downtown Indianapolis, Q Skatepark houses 20,000 square feet of indoor wooden terrain split across distinct street and transition rooms. That physical room separation makes it a forgiving year-round option for older skaters, who can warm up on the dedicated mini-ramp and smaller wooden banks without dodging full-speed street lines. The park also runs weekly one-hour Beginner and Intermediate group lessons for all ages every Wednesday at 6:00pm (as well as weekend mornings at 10:00am and 11:00am and $40 private lessons by appointment), and every lesson includes a full week of free open-skate admission afterward. Its community events even include a dedicated 35-and-over division during Game of S.K.A.T.E. tournaments.

Note that the floor is closed to general open skate during Saturday and Sunday morning group lessons from 10:00am to 12:00pm, and you must RSVP the day before to join a group clinic. Every rider must complete an athletic waiver before stepping onto the course; helmets are mandatory for ages 17 and under and strongly recommended for everyone else ($2 helmet rentals and $3 board rentals are available at the counter, alongside free storage lockers). The park is open to skateboards, inline skates, quad skates, and scooters. If you want a full board evaluation or custom setup on the north side of the metro, stop by [Minus Skate Shop](/directory/shops/us/minus-skateshop-carmel-in) in Carmel, or pair your trip with an outdoor session at [Major Taylor Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/major-taylor-skatepark-indianapolis-in).

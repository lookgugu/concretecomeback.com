Set at 1200 La Crosse Street beside the University of Wisconsin–La Crosse campus, Lueth Park (also known as the Lueth Bicycle and Skate Park) is maintained by the City of La Crosse Parks, Recreation & Forestry Department in partnership with the Outdoor Recreation Alliance (ORA Trails) and the Goosetown Neighborhood Association. Open daily from 6:00am to 10:30pm with evening lighting, shelter space, drinking fountains, and restrooms, the park gives older skaters in western Wisconsin's Driftless Region both quiet early-morning windows and lit evening sessions after work.

What makes Lueth Park especially versatile for returning skaters is its dual-surface layout: alongside the poured-concrete skatepark—which houses a concrete bowl, quarter-pipes, a pyramid, a tabletop, and an 8-foot half-pipe with a roll-in—an underused ballfield was converted in 2018 into a hard-surface, butterfly-shaped asphalt pump track where riders can rebuild carving rhythm and leg stamina without impact or ollies. Keep City of La Crosse operational caveats in mind: the facility is unsupervised, safety gear is mandatory while riding the half-pipe, and the city explicitly notes that the main concrete skatepark transitions are not intended as a nursery for absolute first-time novices—so beginners should warm up on the adjacent asphalt pump track and flat outer approaches during quiet morning hours before dropping into the concrete bowl or half-pipe. Elsewhere in Wisconsin, you can pair a trip across I-90/I-94 with [Irwin A. and Robert D. Goodman Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/goodman-skatepark-madison-wi) in Madison or [Sun Prairie Skateboard Park](/directory/parks/us/sun-prairie-skateboard-park-wi) in Sun Prairie.

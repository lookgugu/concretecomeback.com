Located inside 6.89-acre Sons of Norway Park along East Bismarck Expressway on the south side of town, Sons of Norway Park Skate Park is the most approachable of the three municipal skateparks maintained by the Bismarck Parks and Recreation District (BPRD). While Bismarck's central Lions Park draws heavier traffic to its deeper half-bowl, Sons of Norway Park focuses on lower-consequence poured-concrete obstacles: a short half-pipe (mini-ramp), multiple transition ramps connecting tiered flatground levels, metal copings, and a compact stair feature. For older skaters returning to the board or practicing transition basics, the short half-pipe and gentle level-to-level banks provide a forgiving neighborhood slab with off-street parking, a picnic shelter, and a 0.3-mile paved walking trail right alongside.

Although BPRD park hours run daily from 6:00am to 11:00pm, the skatepark itself does not have dedicated overhead lighting, so sessions are best planned for daylight hours, especially quiet weekday mornings before school lets out. Per BPRD skatepark rules, the unsupervised facility is shared by skateboards, inline skates, and bicycles, helmets and protective pads are strongly recommended, glass containers and motorized vehicles are prohibited, and smoking is barred within 20 feet of park structures. Skaters in North Dakota's capital can pair a warm-up session on the short half-pipe here with the larger transitions across town at [Lions Park Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/lions-park-skate-park-bismarck-nd) or head east along I-94 to [Dike West Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/dike-west-skatepark-fargo-nd) in Fargo.

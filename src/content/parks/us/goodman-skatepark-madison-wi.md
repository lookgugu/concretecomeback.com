Goodman Skatepark is the premier spot in Madison, located within McPike Park. The 20,000-square-foot facility is well-regarded by older skaters for its generous lighting hours (open until 10pm from mid-April to October) and early 4am opening, making it highly practical for those fitting a session around work.

The layout physically separates the street section (which makes up about 70% of the park) from the transition area. The transition section itself offers two distinct bowls: a mellow 4–7 foot flow bowl that is ideal for warming up or regaining confidence, and a more advanced 9-foot eggplant bowl for experienced riders.

Situated at 601 2nd Avenue Northeast in downtown Cullman—directly between the Cullman Police Department and Depot Park just off the Interstate 65 corridor—Skate Depot (also known as the Cullman Skatepark) is an 8,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete municipal facility operated by Cullman Parks, Recreation, and Sports Tourism (CPRST). Designed and built by Spohn Ranch Skateparks and opened in November 2022 through a community-backed funding drive, the park provides a lit, centrally located stop halfway between Birmingham and Huntsville with benches, surrounding landscaping, and public restrooms at adjacent Depot Park.

Spohn Ranch divided the 8,000-square-foot footprint into distinct street and transition zones so returning older skaters can warm up without crossing lines with bowl riders. You can ease into a session along the street plaza's long slappy curb, low manual pads, flat rails, and bank wedges before tackling the stair set and hubba ledges, while transition skaters have a separate dual-level concrete bowl connected by a central transfer spine. Outdoor lighting keeps the concrete rideable until 10:00pm daily, making it practical to skate after work or once Alabama summer afternoon heat fades. Keep a few caveats in mind: Skate Depot is an unsupervised all-wheel facility shared with BMX bikes, scooters, and inline skates, so weekday mornings and later evening hours offer the calmest sessions. When travelling along the I-65 corridor, you can pair a stop in Cullman with [Get-A-Way Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/get-a-way-skatepark-huntsville-al) to the north in Huntsville, the covered overpass concrete at [City Walk Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/city-walk-skatepark-birmingham-al) in downtown Birmingham, or the skateboard-only concrete at [Veterans Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/veterans-skate-park-alabaster-al) in Alabaster.

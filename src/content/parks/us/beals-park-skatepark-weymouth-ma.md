Set between Athens Street, Sea Street, and Bridge Street (Route 3A) in North Weymouth, Beals Park Skatepark is an 18,000-square-foot custom poured-concrete facility designed by New Line Skateparks as part of the Town of Weymouth's multi-million-dollar revitalization of its oldest public park, planned with Activitas. Served by two organized off-street parking lots on Athens Street and Sea Street, a shade pavilion, benches, and paved walking trails, it anchors public concrete skating on the South Shore just south of Boston.

What makes Beals Park particularly welcoming for older skaters returning after time away is its deliberate three-tier ability zoning: New Line divided the 18,000-square-foot slab into distinct beginner, intermediate, and expert areas, allowing riders to practice flatground pushes, gentle banks, low manual pads, and mellow street ledges in the beginner and intermediate sections without crossing into the enclosed expert transition bowl or larger stair and handrail lines. Keep several practical caveats in mind: while the surrounding walking paths have pedestrian lighting open until 11:00pm, the skatepark itself operates on a dawn-to-dusk daylight schedule without dedicated stadium floodlights, dogs and pets are strictly prohibited inside the skatepark enclosure, and warm weekend afternoons draw heavy local scooter and youth traffic—making weekday mornings the calmest window. You can pair a South Shore session here with [Smith Playground Skatepark & Pump Track](/directory/parks/us/smith-playground-skatepark-boston-ma) and [Orchard Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/orchard-skateshop-boston-ma) in Allston, or head under the Zakim Bridge to the covered, floodlit [Lynch Family Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/lynch-family-skatepark-cambridge-ma) in Cambridge.

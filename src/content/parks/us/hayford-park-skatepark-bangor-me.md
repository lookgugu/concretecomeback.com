Located inside Hayford Park at 155 13th Street near Mansfield Stadium and the Union Street athletic complex on Bangor's west side, the Bangor Skatepark is a 7,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete municipal skatepark operated by the City of Bangor Parks and Recreation Department. Designed and built by American Ramp Company across three community-funded phases and opened in September 2021 to replace the city's old wooden ramps ("the slab"), it serves as the anchor public concrete skatepark for Penobscot County and eastern Maine.

The square-shaped concrete pad divides street and transition zones cleanly so returning older skaters can warm up without crossing lines with bowl riders. Along the street plaza, skaters have room to work across manual pads, graduated grind ledges, a flat rail, quarter-pipes, a pyramid A-frame with hubba and rail, and a stair set, while transition skaters have a separate B-shaped flow bowl linked by a central transfer spine. The park also hosts community events on the Maine Skateboard Association circuit. Keep a few caveats in mind before visiting: the skatepark has no dedicated stadium floodlights, so sessions are limited to dawn-to-dusk daylight hours; the park is unsupervised and shared with scooters and BMX bikes during after-school and weekend peaks; and winter snow and ice close the outdoor concrete until spring thaw. When travelling south down I-95, you can pair a Bangor session with [Kennedy Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/kennedy-park-skatepark-lewiston-me) in Lewiston, the indoor wooden ramps at [Bath Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/bath-skatepark-me) in Bath, or [South Portland Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/south-portland-skate-park-me) in Greater Portland.

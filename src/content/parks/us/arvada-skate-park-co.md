The Arvada Skate Park is a massive 40,000-square-foot concrete park located right next to the Apex Center. Its extensive footprint naturally separates different riding zones, meaning returning beginners can comfortably learn in the beginner-friendly mini-ramp and mellow transition areas while advanced skaters use the deep bowls.

Because it operates from sunrise to 10:00 PM with adequate lighting, it is a convenient option for adults fitting in a session before or after the workday. The sheer scale helps thin out the crowds, making it less intimidating for older skaters taking their first drops in years.

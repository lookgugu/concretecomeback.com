Operated by Launch Community Through Skateboarding, this indoor park provides a smooth, birch-surfaced environment safely tucked away from chaotic elements. It features mellow banks, quarter pipes, and an over-vert section suited for learning.

Older skaters will appreciate the facility's strong emphasis on education. They offer private coaching and structured beginner lessons, ensuring adults have a dedicated, low-pressure avenue to build board control and confidence.

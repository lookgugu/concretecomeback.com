Located in Santa Rita Park, the Albert M. Gallego Skate Park is a popular 16,000-square-foot in-ground concrete destination. It's especially practical for older skaters needing to beat the harsh Arizona sun, as the facility is equipped with lights and remains open until 10:30 PM daily.

The park is transition-heavy, featuring three distinct bowls: a Flow Course, a Kidney bowl, and a Clam bowl. While there are some street elements, the flowing transition lines are the main draw. It is an unsupervised facility, so checking the bowls for debris and dust before dropping in is highly recommended.

Set near West 77th Place and Delmar Street inside Prairie Village's Harmon Park just minutes south of downtown Kansas City, Harmon Park Skate Park is a poured-concrete municipal facility redeveloped in 2021 by New Line Skateparks under lead designer and former pro skater Kanten Russell. Created through community outreach to honor the legacy of co-founder Jake Shepard while welcoming every generation of riders, the park opens at 7:00am daily inside a tree-lined civic park equipped with off-street parking, seasonal restrooms, and walking paths.

New Line designed the colored-concrete and brick-accented footprint so older skaters can warm up on low-impact plaza features without crossing high-speed bowl traffic. An integrated stage and event deck doubles as a wide, low manual pad and warmup platform between community gatherings, feeding into approachable banks, brick-trimmed ledges, flat rails, and a stair set with a handrail, while a dedicated concrete bowl occupies its own distinct end of the slab. Keep two operational caveats in mind: although Harmon Park's general municipal hours run from 7:00am to 11:00pm, the skatepark relies on ambient park lighting rather than dedicated high-mast stadium floodlights after dark, and its compact neighborhood footprint fills quickly with school-age skaters after 3:30pm—making weekday mornings the ideal window for returning riders. Before your session, stop by [Escapist Skateboarding](/directory/shops/us/escapist-skateboarding-kansas-city-mo) in Kansas City's Crossroads district for a custom setup, or pair your Johnson County run with push-button evening lights at [Rolling Magic Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/rolling-magic-skate-park-lenexa-ks) in Lenexa and [Penn Valley Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/penn-valley-skatepark-kansas-city-mo) across the state line.

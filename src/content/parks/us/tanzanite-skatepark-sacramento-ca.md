Tanzanite Skatepark offers a balanced array of transition and street elements to accommodate the diverse Sacramento skate scene. It is highly practical for older skaters working typical daytime schedules, offering strong floodlighting and reliable operating hours until 9:00 PM. The park's diverse terrain separates technical ledges from the flow sections, providing space to reacquaint yourself with the board without pressure.

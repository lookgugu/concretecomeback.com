Situated inside Coffman Park near the Dublin Justice Center on the northwest side of the Columbus metro, Dublin Skate Park is a smooth, poured-concrete municipal facility designed and built by Spohn Ranch. The City of Dublin commissioned the layout specifically to accommodate all ages, styles, and experience levels, and equipped the park with overhead floodlighting that keeps the concrete rideable every evening until 11:00pm.

Older skaters easing back into transition or flatground fundamentals can start on the mellow mini-ramp, low banks, and smaller street ledges before dropping into the deeper bowl pockets or stepping up to the stair and rail features. Because this is a free, unsupervised municipal park open to both skateboards and inline skates, after-school hours on fair-weather afternoons can get busy; early mornings after dawn and late evening sessions under the lights offer the calmest windows to work on lines at your own pace. For another lit Spohn Ranch concrete facility on the east side of the Columbus region—complete with a concrete pump track and tiered bowls—pair a session here with [Everett Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/everett-park-skatepark-newark-oh) in Newark.

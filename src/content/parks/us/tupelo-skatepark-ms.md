The Tupelo Skatepark, situated in Ballard Park, is a versatile concrete facility with a layout that works well for progression. The main bowl features varying depths, allowing newer transition riders to start small before moving to the deeper ends, while the street section offers standard ledges and rails.

What makes this park particularly friendly for older skaters is its lighting system. The lights are user-activated via a button near the restrooms and stay on until 12:30 am, providing a fantastic opportunity for late-night sessions when the park is completely empty.

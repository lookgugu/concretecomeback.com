Set directly behind the Nienhuis Park Community Center and Aquatic Facility in Broken Arrow, Nienhuis Skate Park spans roughly 30,000 square feet of free municipal concrete after a major 2021 expansion that more than doubled its original 2007 footprint. Operated by the City of Broken Arrow Parks and Recreation Department and floodlit daily from 7:00am to 11:00pm year-round, the facility gives older skaters in the eastern Tulsa metro reliable early-morning and late-evening windows supported by adjacent parking, picnic pavilions, and park restrooms.

The multi-phase layout naturally separates ability levels and riding styles: a dedicated concrete pump track extension designed by Hunger Skateparks sits at one end for low-impact pumping and carving, a sprawling street plaza offers wide flatground lanes, wedge banks, manual pads, and progressive grind ledges in the middle, and the original 6-foot-deep concrete bowl and quarter-pipe section anchors the opposite side. Because the park welcomes BMX bikes, scooters, and inline skates alongside skateboards and is unsupervised, weekday mornings right after 7:00am or later evening hours under the lights offer the most relaxed sessions. You can easily pair a suburb session here with [Gathering Place Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/gathering-place-skate-park-tulsa-ok) along the Arkansas River in Tulsa.

Glasgow Skate Park is a substantial 24,000-square-foot public concrete facility set within Glasgow Regional Park. It features a varied street section with hubba ledges and banked wall rides, alongside two different styles of bowls. 

The park is praised for its smooth, well-maintained surface, making it an excellent spot for learning and progression. While it can get busy on weekends, older skaters consistently recommend weekday morning and early afternoon hours for a quiet, low-pressure session. The varied features allow skaters to pick obstacles perfectly matched to their current comfort level. For a smaller, low-consequence warmup spot right in Newark, you can start at the [Norma B. Handloff Park Skate Spot](/directory/parks/us/handloff-park-skate-spot-newark-de) on Barksdale Road, or head downstate to the Delaware beaches to ride [Epworth Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/epworth-skatepark-rehoboth-beach-de) in Rehoboth Beach.

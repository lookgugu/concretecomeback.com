Built with support from the Tony Hawk Foundation, Riverside Skatepark offers high-quality concrete and sweeping views of the Ambassador Bridge. The sheer size of the 30,000 square foot layout means that beginners can find quiet spots to practice flatground tricks without crossing paths with the fast-paced clover bowl traffic. Its long operating hours and reliable lighting make it incredibly accessible for those who need to skate around a 9-to-5 schedule.

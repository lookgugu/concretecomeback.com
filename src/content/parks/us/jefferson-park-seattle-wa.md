Jefferson Park Skatepark is a highly regarded spot in Seattle that caters to a wide range of styles. While the main bowl is notably deep and better suited for experienced riders, the park also features a fantastic flow section with a small spine and forgiving hips that are great for older skaters finding their groove.

The park's extended hours are a major selling point. Lights stay on until 11:30 PM, and it opens as early as 4:00 AM, guaranteeing that you can find a quiet window to practice outside of peak traffic times.

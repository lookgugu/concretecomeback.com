Tucked inside Jackie Renninger Park along Mendenhall Loop Road in Juneau's Mendenhall Valley, the Pipeline Skate Park is a covered, open-sided municipal skate facility managed by City and Borough of Juneau (CBJ) Parks and Recreation in partnership with the local Skate Board advisory group. Because Juneau averages more than 200 days of precipitation a year, the park's full overhead roof canopy is its defining draw for older skaters—keeping the wood ramps, mini-ramp transitions, banks, rails, and underlying concrete foundation dry enough to fit reliable sessions around work and Southeast Alaska weather.

Returning riders can warm up on the flat concrete approaches, low ledges, mellow banks, and smaller quarter-pipe transitions under the canopy before working up to the steeper ramp walls. Keep several CBJ operational rules (CBJ 67.01) and seasonal caveats in mind: the facility is unsupervised and self-policed, and its perimeter gates are locked outside active seasonal operating windows (typically weekday afternoons/evenings and 8:00am–8:00pm on weekends, though CBJ adjusts gate schedules seasonally—check before driving out). Hard-shell knee pads, elbow pads, and a helmet are strongly recommended by city code (even for spectators standing inside the active riding area), amplified music requires a special permit, and riding is prohibited whenever blowing snow, ice, or cold-weather condensation makes the covered ramps slick. Elsewhere in Alaska, you can compare it with the covered, LED-lit [Turnaround Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/turnaround-skate-park-sitka-ak) in Sitka or the outdoor concrete at [Taku Lake Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/taku-lake-skatepark-anchorage-ak) in Anchorage.

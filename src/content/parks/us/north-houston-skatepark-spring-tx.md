North Houston Skatepark is one of the largest skateparks in North America, and that sheer scale is its biggest asset for older skaters. Even on busy days, the sprawling 77,000-square-foot footprint offers enough low-traffic areas to comfortably practice your basics. 

The layout intelligently separates skill levels, featuring a forgiving beginner flow bowl that keeps you safely out of the way of the competition-scale vert and street sections. It is free to use and heavily lit for night sessions. Note that the park is traditionally closed on Tuesdays and requires helmets for all riders.

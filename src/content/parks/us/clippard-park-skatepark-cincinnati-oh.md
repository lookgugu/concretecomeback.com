Clippard Park Skatepark is a solid concrete facility in the greater Cincinnati area that caters well to older skaters returning to the sport. The layout offers clear ability separation between its transition features.

The highlight for those regaining their confidence is a mellow 4-foot bowl with a ledge, which is kept physically distinct from the daunting 10-foot advanced bowl with pool coping. The street section includes a staircase, ramp, and railings, providing a balanced mix for all riding styles.

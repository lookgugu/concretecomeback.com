Located inside 84.5-acre Tautphaus Park along South Boulevard, Tautphaus Park Skatepark (officially listed by the city as the Idaho Falls Skate Park) is a cast-in-place concrete facility designed and built by Grindline Skateparks and maintained by the City of Idaho Falls Parks & Recreation Department. Open daily from 5:00am until 11:00pm with overhead evening lighting and a strict municipal ban on motorized vehicles, e-bikes, and e-scooters, it serves as eastern Idaho's primary all-concrete hub for older skaters looking for quiet dawn roll-ins or lit weeknight sessions after work.

Grindline organized the layout into two distinct zones so returning riders can warm up on manageable street features before testing steeper transitions. You can start on the open street course—working across flatground approaches, launch ramps, a central pyramid, low grind ledges, stairs, and handrails—before stepping into the concrete transition bowl, which links metal-coped pockets and hips into an over-vertical capsule cradle. Keep a few local caveats in mind: because the skatepark sits inside Idaho Falls' busiest regional park alongside the Idaho Falls Zoo and Funland Amusement Park, summer weekend afternoons draw heavy family and scooter traffic, making the 5:00am–9:00am morning window or late evening lights the calmest times to ride; and at 4,700 feet of elevation in the Snake River Plain, winter snow and ice regularly pause outdoor sessions. If you are travelling across southern Idaho toward the Treasure Valley, pair your session with [Rhodes Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/rhodes-skatepark-boise-id) in Boise, [Tully Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/tully-park-skatepark-meridian-id) in Meridian, and a shop stop at [Prestige Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/prestige-skateshop-boise-id).

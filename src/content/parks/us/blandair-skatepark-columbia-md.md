Blandair Skatepark opened in June 2026 in the west area of Blandair Regional Park, off Oakland Mills Road in Columbia. At 15,000 square feet it is the largest skatepark in Howard County and the fourth public skate facility run by Howard County Recreation & Parks. The county describes a modern concrete design built into the site's slope and meant for a variety of wheeled sports, riding styles, terrain preferences and skill levels.

The reason it suits older skaters is the lighting. Howard County lists the skatepark as lighted, which makes evening sessions around a working day possible. The same project added 122 parking spaces, and a shaded seating area overlooks the park for breaks between runs. Trucks and Fins lists banks, quarter pipes, a mini ramp, ledges, a manual pad, stairs with a handrail, a wallride and a bowl. If you are rebuilding confidence, start on the banks, manual pad and mini ramp before the bowl.

Two caveats. The county also calls this a competition-level skatepark and it is open to bikes and scooters, so expect fast traffic at peak times. Published park hours are 7am to dusk or as posted, and no source we read gives the skatepark's lighting schedule, so check the county site or the signs on site before planning a late session.

For more sessions nearby, [Skatepark of Baltimore](/directory/parks/us/skatepark-of-baltimore-md) and [Hazelwood Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/hazelwood-skatepark-baltimore-md) are to the northeast, and [Olney Manor Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/olney-manor-skate-park-md) and [Ovid Hazen Wells Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/ovid-hazen-wells-skate-park-clarksburg-md) are to the west in Montgomery County.

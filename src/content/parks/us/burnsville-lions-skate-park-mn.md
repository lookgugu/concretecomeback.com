Located inside Civic Center Park adjacent to Burnsville City Hall between 130th and 134th Streets, the Burnsville Lions Skate Park is an 11,500-square-foot outdoor poured-concrete facility operated by the City of Burnsville in the south Twin Cities metro. Open daily from 5:00am to 10:00pm, the park gives older skaters quiet early-morning windows before suburban traffic builds, along with practical on-site amenities including a dedicated skateboard fixing station for hardware tune-ups, an adjacent flat inline skating rink for warmups, a covered picnic shelter, a drinking fountain, and portable restrooms.

The concrete footprint separates its street and bank course—featuring quarter-pipes, transition hips, a China bank, grind ledges and boxes, a spine ramp, and a 6-stair set with a handrail and step-up—from an amoeba-shaped concrete bowl with an extension, letting returning riders work on flatground and low ledges away from bowl lines. Keep several City of Burnsville caveats in mind: the park is an unsupervised "skate at your own risk" facility shared with inline skates, scooters, and bikes; it has no dedicated stadium floodlighting (relying on surrounding Civic Center Park lighting after dusk); it is unmaintained during Minnesota's winter snow season; and Burnsville parks enforce a "Bring It In, Take It Out" trash policy alongside strict bans on glass containers and alcohol. When winter weather hits the south metro, you can head indoors to [3rd Lair Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/3rd-lair-skatepark-golden-valley-mn) in Golden Valley or [Familia Headquarters](/directory/parks/us/familia-headquarters-minneapolis-mn) and [Familia Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/familia-skateshop-minneapolis-mn) in Minneapolis.

As Wisconsin's largest outdoor poured-concrete facility, Joannes Skate Park offers an expansive layout that naturally diffuses heavy traffic. 

For older skaters, the primary draw is its intentional physical separation of skill levels. The park features dedicated beginner areas far removed from the advanced transition sections, giving you a low-impact zone to build foundational board control without dodging high-speed riders.

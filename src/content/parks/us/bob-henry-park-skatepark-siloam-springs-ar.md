Set along Sager Creek inside Bob Henry Park at 500 West Benton Street, the Siloam Springs Skatepark is a free municipal facility managed by the City of Siloam Springs Parks and Recreation Department on the western edge of Northwest Arkansas. Equipped with stadium lighting that keeps the concrete illuminated until 11:00pm alongside paved parking, a large covered pavilion, and seasonal park restrooms, it offers older skaters dependable evening sessions after work and family commitments wrap up.

The facility is split across two adjacent poured-concrete pads that let returning older skaters separate warm-up progression from steeper transition. The primary concrete plaza features a mini-ramp, quarter-pipes, flat and round flatbars, a pyramid funbox, a four-stair with handrail, and a series of ledges built in graduated heights from ankle-high to tall so riders can work up to grinds incrementally. In October 2024, the city and contractor Primary Concrete completed a Phase I renovation on the neighboring pad, replacing the park's aging early-2000s metal modular ramps with new poured-concrete quarter-pipes and transition banks. Keep a few caveats in mind: the park is unsupervised and shared with scooters and bikes during late-afternoon peak hours; low-lying creekside concrete can collect leaves or damp spots after heavy Ozark rain; and park restrooms are winterized during freezing weather. If you are skating across Northwest Arkansas and the River Valley, pair a session here with [Memorial Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/memorial-park-skatepark-bentonville-ar) in Bentonville, [Riverfront Skate and Bike Park](/directory/parks/us/riverfront-skate-and-bike-park-fort-smith-ar) in Fort Smith, or [Riverview Skateboard Park](/directory/parks/us/riverview-skateboard-park-north-little-rock-ar) in North Little Rock.

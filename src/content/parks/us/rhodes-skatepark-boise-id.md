Rhodes Skatepark is a massive, world-class concrete facility located in downtown Boise. Situated underneath the I-184 connector overpass, the park is partially covered, which helps the smooth concrete surface stay dry during light rain and extends the riding season. 

While the park boasts large bowls and professional-level street plazas, it was built with a "Ladder Progression" philosophy. Older skaters will appreciate the dedicated beginner area, deliberately designed so newer riders can practice the basics away from the advanced, high-speed traffic of the main flow zones. Motorized vehicles and alcohol are strictly prohibited. For gear and safety equipment, stop by [Prestige Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/prestige-skateshop-boise-id) downtown.

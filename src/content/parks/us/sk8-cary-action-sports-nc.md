Sk8-Cary provides a structured, supportive environment that is rare for municipal parks. Built primarily with wood frames and Skatelite Pro surfaces, it caters explicitly to older skaters by reserving specific 18+ adult sessions, ensuring you have time to ride without younger traffic. 

The facility is supervised and requires a waiver as well as full safety gear (helmets and pads). With an on-site pro shop and accessible beginner ramps alongside more challenging street features, it’s an excellent home base for easing back into the sport. Check their official town page for the most current session hours.

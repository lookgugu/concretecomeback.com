Serving the greater Burlington area, Talent Skatepark is an ~8,300 square foot climate-controlled indoor facility. It features a varied layout, including a plaza section, transition bowl, and micro mini-ramp, all surfaced with smooth wood.

The park strongly caters to the adult and returning skater demographic. They run highly structured clinics with professional "SKoaches" and host dedicated sessions specifically for those aged 25 and older, ensuring that you can learn without dodging the typical high-speed crowds.

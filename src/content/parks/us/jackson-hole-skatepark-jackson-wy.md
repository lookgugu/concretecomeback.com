Located beside Jackson Hole High School athletic fields under the shadow of the Teton Range, this municipal concrete park underwent a major modern expansion designed and poured by Grindline Skateparks. For older skaters, the newer additions represent a welcome shift toward accessible, low-impact riding: a forgiving wooden-radius concrete mini-ramp, Pier 7-style manual pads, low flat rails, and wide banked hips that let returners ease back into flow.

The layout cleanly segregates the beginner-friendly street terrace from the older, deep-end cloverleaf bowl, eliminating collisions with high-speed bowl carvers. Early mornings right at sunrise deliver calm sessions with crisp mountain air before local youth arrive. Keep in mind that Jackson sits at 6,237 feet elevation, so older skaters should pace their endurance, hydrate frequently, and dress for swift mountain temperature drops in autumn.

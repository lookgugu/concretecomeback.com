Set at the corner of Blackburn Avenue and Cougar Avenue inside Mentock Park (1901 Blackburn Avenue) on the northwest side of Cody, the Mentock Park Skatepark is an 8,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete municipal skatepark operated by the City of Cody Parks and Recreation Department. Sharing a neighborhood park complex with public restrooms, a covered Rotary Club picnic shelter, a playground, a seasonal splash pad, and off-street parking, it serves as the primary concrete skatepark in Wyoming's Bighorn Basin near the eastern gateway to Yellowstone.

According to the City of Cody's official facility directory, the 8,000-square-foot concrete pad is intentionally divided into three distinct zones: "a multi-use area for beginners, street course, and signature area containing a 9-foot bowl for more advanced skateboarders." That physical separation lets returning older skaters warm up on flatground and low street banks, ledges, and rails in the beginner and street sections without crossing lines with fast bowl riders dropping into the 9-foot deep end. Note a few practical caveats before visiting: the skatepark has no evening floodlights, so riding is restricted to dawn-to-dusk daylight hours; the park is unsupervised and shared with scooters and bikes during summer afternoons; and Mentock Park's restrooms and water utilities are winterized from October through late spring. Skaters road-tripping across Wyoming can pair a Bighorn Basin session here with [Sheridan Community Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/sheridan-community-skate-park-wy) over the Bighorn Mountains, [Jackson Hole Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/jackson-hole-skatepark-jackson-wy) in Jackson, or [LaBonte Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/labonte-skate-park-laramie-wy) in Laramie.

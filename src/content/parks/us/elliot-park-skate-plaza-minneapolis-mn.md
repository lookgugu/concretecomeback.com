Located at 1000 East 14th Street on the east side of downtown Minneapolis—two blocks south of U.S. Bank Stadium—Elliot Park Skate Plaza is the largest outdoor skatepark in the Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board (MPRB) system. Developed in partnership with City of Skate, X Games, and California Skateparks as part of a comprehensive park renovation, the custom-poured concrete plaza sits alongside an athletic field, rain gardens, a permeable paver patio, and the Elliot Park Recreation Center. Standard MPRB park hours run from 6:00am to midnight daily, with upgraded pedestrian pathway lighting and adjacent athletic field lighting supporting early-morning and after-work sessions.

For older skaters looking for a low-consequence outdoor warm-up spot in the city core, the plaza's wide, single-level layout emphasizes long flatground lines and approachable street obstacles rather than deep bowls. You can start along the smooth concrete apron, skateable seat walls, and low manual ledges before working onto the flat rails, banks, and perimeter quarter-pipe, with generous viewing and resting benches along the paver patio. Keep a few caveats in mind before visiting: the park is entirely street-plaza oriented with no deep transition bowls; its downtown neighborhood setting draws lively after-school crowds and heavy stadium traffic on event days; and Minnesota winter snow and ice close the outdoor concrete for several months each year. Before rolling out—or whenever winter weather drives sessions indoors—visit [Familia Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/familia-skateshop-minneapolis-mn) and its attached indoor park [Familia Headquarters](/directory/parks/us/familia-headquarters-minneapolis-mn) in Northeast Minneapolis, book a Wednesday 30+ night at [3rd Lair Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/3rd-lair-skatepark-golden-valley-mn) in Golden Valley, or head south down I-35W to [Burnsville Lions Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/burnsville-lions-skate-park-mn).

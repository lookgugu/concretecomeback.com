Set inside 23.8-acre Norma B. Handloff Park along Barksdale Road on the west side of Newark, the Norma B. Handloff Park Skate Spot (often called the Barksdale Skate Spot) is a 2,800-square-foot poured-in-place concrete facility designed and built by Grindline Skateparks for the City of Newark Parks and Recreation Department. For older skaters in northern Delaware who want to rebuild board feel without stepping immediately into a massive regional bowl complex, its compact neighborhood scale and low-profile obstacles offer a calm, approachable alternative with on-site parking, a drinking fountain, and seasonal park restrooms.

Grindline shaped the slab around smooth flatground approaches bookended by two mellow quarter-pipes, bank ramps, a hip, a China bank, and a low hubba ledge—ideal for practicing kickturns, slappies, basic ledge grinds, and pumping speed at a manageable pace. Keep a few City of Newark operational caveats in mind: the skate spot is unlit and open strictly from dawn to dusk daily, bringing or constructing portable ramps or standalone rails is prohibited by city code, and afternoon school dismissal near adjacent Barksdale Park fields can crowd the compact footprint—making weekday mornings the best time for a quiet solo session. When you are ready for deeper bowls and a larger street plaza in the same area, pair a warmup here with nearby 24,000-square-foot [Glasgow Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/glasgow-skate-park-newark-de) on Pulaski Highway, or head downstate to [Epworth Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/epworth-skatepark-rehoboth-beach-de) in Rehoboth Beach.

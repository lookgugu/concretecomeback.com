Overlooking the Arkansas River at 2801 River Road beside the Arkansas River Trail inside Riverview Park, Riverview Skateboard Park is a 16,000-square-foot custom poured-concrete facility designed and built by Dreamland Skateparks for North Little Rock Parks and Recreation. Open daily from 6:00am until 30 minutes after sunset with adjacent riverfront parking, the park is the premier public concrete alternative in the Little Rock metro for older skaters—especially because municipal rules strictly prohibit bicycles on the slab to prevent metal pegs from chipping the coping and eliminate high-speed bike collisions.

Dreamland's layout separates an expansive street course—featuring three gentle roll-ins, bank ramps, pyramids, funboxes, low grind ledges, and flat rails—from its centerpiece U-shaped concrete bowl and spine, which begins at a forgiving 3-foot shallow end before dropping to a 10-to-12-foot vertical deep pocket. That progressive 3-foot entry makes it much more approachable for returning transition skaters than Little Rock's advanced-only Kanis Bowl across the river. Keep a few operational caveats in mind: while protective pads are not mandated by city rule, helmets and knee pads are strongly recommended for the deep end, river humidity and morning dew can leave the smooth concrete slick right after dawn, and general park gates close 30 minutes after sunset so sessions should be planned for daylight hours. Skaters travelling up the I-40 and I-49 corridor can pair a central Arkansas session here with [Riverfront Skate and Bike Park](/directory/parks/us/riverfront-skate-and-bike-park-fort-smith-ar) in Fort Smith or [Memorial Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/memorial-park-skatepark-bentonville-ar) in Bentonville.

Set inside the 17-acre remediated grounds of Berry Lane Park in Jersey City's Bergen-Lafayette neighborhood, Berry Lane Skatepark is a 12,000-square-foot cast-in-place concrete facility designed by SITE Design Group and funded in partnership with the Jersey City Redevelopment Agency, the Hudson County Open Space Trust Fund, and The Skatepark Project. As the first poured-in-place concrete skatepark in Hudson County, the park sits directly adjacent to a 2,000-square-foot park pavilion equipped with public restrooms, benches, walkways, and bike racks, with direct transit access from the Garfield Avenue Hudson-Bergen Light Rail station and nearby Citi Bike docks.

SITE Design Group organized the slab so older skaters can warm up on low-consequence terrain before dropping into deeper transition. One side of the park features a dedicated backyard-style bowl with a clamshell pocket, a built-in bank, and an extended shallow quarter-pipe section that can be ridden back and forth like a mini-ramp, while the street plaza stretches out across flatground corridors with a pump bump, roller, layback bank, hipped quarter-pipes, a bump-to-ledge, an A-frame ledge combo, a split-level A-frame with gap, a flat rail over a gap, and a 3-stair rail. Keep a few operational caveats in mind: the skatepark is an unlit dawn-to-dusk facility without dedicated overhead floodlighting on the concrete, operates as an unsupervised all-wheel park where bikes and scooters share the lines during busy after-school hours, and is best visited on weekday mornings for calm sessions. You can easily pair a Hudson County session here with [Sayreville Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/sayreville-skatepark-nj) down the Turnpike, or hop across the Hudson River into Manhattan to visit [Labor Skateshop](/directory/shops/us/labor-skateshop-new-york-ny) and [Pier 62 Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/pier-62-skatepark-new-york-ny).

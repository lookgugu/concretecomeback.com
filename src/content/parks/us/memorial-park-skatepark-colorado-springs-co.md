Located near East Pikes Peak Avenue and South Hancock Avenue inside Colorado Springs' flagship municipal park, Memorial Park Skatepark is a 40,000-to-45,000-square-foot custom concrete facility designed and built by Team Pain for Colorado Springs Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services. Opening daily at 5:00am with overhead floodlighting that keeps the course skateable until 10:00pm from May through October (and 9:00pm from November through April), the complex gives older skaters along the southern Front Range quiet sunrise sessions and reliable after-work windows with views of Pikes Peak.

Team Pain split the massive footprint in half so returning skaters can warm up without getting tangled in heavy transition traffic: a full 20,000-square-foot street plaza offers wide flatground approaches, mellow banks, quarter-pipes, ledges, hubbas, stairs, and handrails, while the transition half houses multi-depth flow bowls, a signature "hole-in-the-wall" feature, a backyard-style pool, and a maintained 6-foot wooden ramp. Keep two local operational updates in mind before visiting: in February 2026 the city permanently decommissioned and removed the deteriorating 14-foot wooden vertical half-pipe (while keeping the smaller 6-foot ramp open), and effective September 2026 Memorial Park's 19 vehicle security gates close at 7:00pm every Sunday—drivers must exit the lots before 7:00pm on Sundays, though pedestrian and skateboard access continues until regular park closing. If you are travelling north up I-25 toward the Denver metro, pair your session with [Denver Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/denver-skatepark-co), [Arvada Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/arvada-skate-park-co), and a gear stop at [303 Boards](/directory/shops/us/303-boards-denver-co).

Situated on the campus of the Indy Cycloplex, Major Taylor Skatepark is a concrete facility designed to encourage progression. The park includes a variety of street elements, bowls, and half-pipes that cater to advanced riders, but its layout provides enough space for learning the basics.

As a popular public park, it can get busy during peak hours. However, sources note a reliable quiet window during weekday mornings, offering older beginners a much less intimidating environment to practice without navigating heavy traffic. Proper safety gear—including helmets, knee pads, and elbow pads—is required for all users. For board evaluations, repairs, or custom builds, [Minus Skate Shop](/directory/shops/us/minus-skateshop-carmel-in) in nearby Carmel is highly recommended.

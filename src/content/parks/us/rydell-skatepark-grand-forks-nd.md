Rydell Skatepark, located in Kannowski Park, is a community staple that received a major concrete resurfacing and revitalization in 2023. The layout blends an open bowl with fundamental street features.

The park is widely celebrated as beginner-friendly and safe, designed specifically to be approachable for new riders while still allowing room for progression. With operating hours starting early at 6am and lights keeping the park open until 11pm, older skaters have plenty of flexibility to find a quiet window that fits their schedule.

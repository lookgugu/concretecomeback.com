Situated along North Last Chance Gulch inside 58-acre Centennial/Bausch Park beside the Helena Family YMCA, Helena Skate Park is a 17,000-square-foot poured-concrete municipal facility operated by the City of Helena Parks, Recreation and Open Lands Department. For older skaters in Montana's state capital, the park's biggest draw is its posted wheel policy: the concrete is reserved for skateboarders and inline skaters, with both scooters and BMX bikes explicitly prohibited—eliminating unpredictable scooter cross-traffic during warmup runs.

Built across two major phases, the layout separates a spacious street plaza from deep bowl transition. Returning skaters can start in the original street section, which surrounds an open flatground floor with quarter-pipe corners, flat-bank hips, two ledge gaps, a central pyramid with a square tube rail, and five distinct skateable benches (one curved, one sloping, one with a top step, one standard ledge, and one fitted with a 2-foot-high rail), before progressing into the expansion zone's two large concrete bowls, stairs, banks, and handrails. Keep a few practical caveats in mind: the park is an unlit outdoor facility limited to daylight sessions, operates unsupervised, and experiences extended snow and freeze-thaw closures through the winter months at Helena's 4,000-foot Rocky Mountain elevation. Skaters traveling across western and central Montana can pair a capital-city session with [MOBASH Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/mobash-skatepark-missoula-mt) in Missoula or [Downtown Billings Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/downtown-billings-skate-park-mt) in Billings.

Operated by the nonprofit Midcoast Youth Center at 4 Old Brunswick Road, Bath Skatepark is the largest indoor skatepark in Maine, housing more than 6,000 square feet of wooden ramps, quarter-pipes, ledges, flat bars, and boxes inside a climate-controlled warehouse. For older skaters in Midcoast and southern Maine, the timber surfaces offer lower-impact landings than cold outdoor concrete and keep sessions going through months of snow and rain.

Two policies make the facility particularly welcoming for returning riders: after 6:00pm on open evenings, floor access is restricted to skaters aged 16 and up (unless accompanied by a parent or guardian), calming the floor on Friday and Saturday nights, and one-hour private skateboarding lessons ($25) can be booked in advance for one-on-one confidence building. Helmet and skateboard rentals ($4–$5) are also available at the desk. Note that Saturday mornings from 10:00am to 12:00pm are reserved for a "Half Pints" session limited to children ages 6–10, and general sessions are shared with scooters, inline skates, and BMX bikes.

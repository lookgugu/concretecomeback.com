Set in West Thorne-Rider Park along the east side of Dana Avenue near West 12th Street beneath the Bighorn Mountains, the Sheridan Community Skate Park is a free public poured-concrete facility maintained jointly by the Sheridan Recreation District and the City of Sheridan. Unlike Wyoming's unlit high-country parks, Thorne-Rider is equipped with overhead lighting for evening sessions alongside off-street parking, picnic shelters, athletic fields, and seasonal park restrooms, giving older skaters in northern Wyoming practical after-work windows in addition to quiet morning roll-ins.

The concrete course separates its open street and flow area—stocked with flatground approaches, bank ramps, grind blocks, flat rails, and a mini-ramp transition zone with a spine and small platform—from a deeper concrete bowl, allowing returning riders to regain board control on low-impact obstacles before dropping into steeper transition. Keep Sheridan Recreation District rules and caveats in mind: helmets and safety pads are required at all times, the park is unsupervised and shared across wheeled disciplines, and evening skaters must respect local municipal noise laws and park curfews. Skaters touring Wyoming can pair a northern Bighorn session here with [Jackson Hole Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/jackson-hole-skatepark-jackson-wy) in Jackson or [LaBonte Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/labonte-skate-park-laramie-wy) in Laramie.

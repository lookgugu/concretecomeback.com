Founded in Saskatoon in 1998, Ninetimes is a cornerstone of the Canadian prairie skateboarding scene. As an independent, skater-owned storefront on Louise Avenue, the shop has spent more than two decades supporting the local community with deep inventory across core deck brands, trucks, wheels, safety accessories, and footwear.

The shop operates Monday to Friday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday from 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and Sunday from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM. Whether you are building a wider cruiser setup or dialing in softer wheels to absorb street vibration, the staff provide knowledgeable, pressure-free advice. Lions SkatePark in Victoria Park is located a short drive west along 8th Street and Spadina Crescent.

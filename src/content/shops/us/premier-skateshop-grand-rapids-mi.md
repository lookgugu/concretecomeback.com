Premier Skateshop is a premium core shop located in downtown Grand Rapids. While they also function as a high-end sneaker and streetwear boutique, their roots in skateboarding remain strong, and they stock professional-grade gear that will save a beginner from the frustration of a poor-quality setup.

Customer reviews consistently highlight the staff's friendly and patient approach. They are happy to walk older or returning skaters through the nuances of different decks and trucks without any pressure. While in town, check out the nearby **555 Monroe Skatepark** for a smooth, low-impact practice session.

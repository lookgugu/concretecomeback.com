Southside is a short drive south of downtown Houston and a different proposition from a street-front shop: the counter is inside the skatepark, so advice comes from people who will watch you ride what they sold you. Hours follow the park — Monday to Thursday 2pm to 9pm, Friday until 10pm, Saturday 11am to 9pm and Sunday 1pm to 9pm — and the online store ships across the US with free shipping over a spend threshold if you would rather browse from home first.

Located in Carmel, just north of Indianapolis, Minus Skate Shop is renowned for its strong community focus and hands-on service. They stock complete kits, build-your-own decks, and longboards, ensuring they can equip everyone from first-timers to life-long skaters.

The shop is particularly valuable for returning skaters due to its comprehensive repair and maintenance services. The staff routinely perform full board evaluations—checking bearings, wheels, and deck condition—and are happy to assist with custom board builds. It's a great spot to get your setup dialed in before hitting [Major Taylor Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/major-taylor-skatepark-indianapolis-in) down the road.

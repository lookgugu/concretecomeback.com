Situated directly within the [Fargo Skateboarding](/directory/parks/us/fargo-skateboarding-dekalb-il) indoor facility, this shop provides a wide selection of skateboards, completes, and protective gear. 

The shop doubles as the registration point for the facility's extensive training programs. They coordinate one-on-one private lessons and run structured group classes specifically designed for adults, ensuring that returning skaters have access to professional coaching in a supportive, heated indoor environment.

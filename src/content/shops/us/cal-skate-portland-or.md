Cal Skate predates most of the words we use for skateboarding, and it is still on NW 6th Avenue downtown. When ownership passed to Chris Nukala after nearly fifty years, local news covered it as a passing of the torch: "I got my first board at Cal Skate when I was eight years old," he said, "the experience changed my life." That continuity is what a returning skater is buying into. Check current hours before you go — they were not published at the time of listing — and pair the visit with Burnside, the DIY park under the bridge that Portland is known for.

No-Comply carries the new-school brands alongside the ones you will remember — Alien Workshop and Baker sit next to Limosine and Opera — and will build a complete to suit you rather than sell you a pre-made one. Hours are 11am to 7pm Monday to Friday, 10am to 7pm Saturday and 10am to 6pm Sunday, and the online store ships worldwide. Heath Eiland and Mabel Davis parks are both in easy reach.

Orchard has anchored the Boston skate scene since 2006. In 2025, the shop moved into a massive new flagship space in Allston, continuing their legacy of community building.

Far from the gatekeeping sometimes found in core shops, Orchard's ownership has a documented history of making sure their spaces—including their famous indoor mini-ramps—are comfortable for older, returning skaters to learn on. It's an essential stop if you need gear before rolling down the street to [Smith Playground Skatepark & Pump Track](/directory/parks/us/smith-playground-skatepark-boston-ma) in Allston, heading across the river to [Lynch Family Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/lynch-family-skatepark-cambridge-ma) in Cambridge, or driving down to [Beals Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/beals-park-skatepark-weymouth-ma) in Weymouth and [Scituate Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/scituate-skatepark-ma) on the South Shore.

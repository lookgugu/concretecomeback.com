Escapist Skateboarding is widely regarded as the cornerstone of the Kansas City skate scene. Rather than pushing intimidating core vibes, the skater-owned shop focuses heavily on community and support for all skill levels. 

Customers rave about the staff's willingness to patiently explain equipment differences to beginners and older skaters getting back on a board. If you're heading to the nearby [Penn Valley Skatepark](/directory/parks/us/penn-valley-skatepark-kansas-city-mo), crossing into Johnson County for [Harmon Park Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/harmon-park-skate-park-prairie-village-ks) in Prairie Village or [Rolling Magic Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/rolling-magic-skate-park-lenexa-ks) in Lenexa, driving west on I-70 for lit evening sessions at [Centennial Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/centennial-skate-park-lawrence-ks) in Lawrence, or heading east along I-70 to [Columbia Skate Park](/directory/parks/us/columbia-skate-park-cosmo-park-mo) at Cosmo Park in Columbia, stopping here first for a custom build or fresh gear is highly recommended.

Owner Tony Croghan opened 35th North in 2001, and the shop has grown from a tiny University District room into one of the city's best-known skate stores. It is open 11am to 8pm Monday through Saturday and 11am to 5pm on Sunday, with discounted shipping for Washington residents and free shipping over $100 if you order online. Lower Woodland is the obvious park to pair it with.

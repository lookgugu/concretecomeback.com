Evolve is Melbourne's longest-running independent skate shop and an institution in the local scene. Despite its deep roots and core pedigree, the shop is famous for being incredibly welcoming.

Whether you're stepping on a board for the first time in twenty years or need help figuring out modern truck sizing, customers consistently report that the staff are patient, knowledgeable, and completely lack the intimidating "cooler-than-you" attitude sometimes found in core shops. It's perfectly positioned on Chapel Street for grabbing gear before rolling down to Prahran Skate Park.

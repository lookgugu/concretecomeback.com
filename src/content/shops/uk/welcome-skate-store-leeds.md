Welcome sits in Thorntons Arcade in the city centre, a fifteen-minute roll from Hyde Park Skatepark on Woodhouse Moor. The consistent thread in customer reviews is the staff: helpful with setup questions, welcoming to complete beginners, and not fussed if you leave without buying anything. Hours are 11am to 6pm Monday, 10am to 6pm Tuesday to Saturday and 11am to 5pm Sunday, and the online shop offers next-working-day delivery across the UK if you would rather arrive at the park already set up.

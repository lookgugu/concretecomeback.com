Jamie and Davie Philip opened Clan Skates in 1988 while skating professionally, and the shop has stayed rider-owned ever since — which is why it has outlasted every other skate shop in Scotland. It stocks snowboards, scooters and surf gear alongside skateboards, and offers skateboard repairs, not just sales. Jamie also turns up in the story of Kelvin Wheelies, the 1978 concrete park whose remains sit beside the modern Kelvingrove skatepark, so it is the right place to ask about Glasgow's skate history as well as a new setup. Check the website for current opening hours.

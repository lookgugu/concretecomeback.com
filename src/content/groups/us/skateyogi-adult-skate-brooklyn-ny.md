SKATEYOGI runs dedicated evening open skate sessions and multi-week coaching series tailored specifically for older skaters and beginner returners. Hosted indoors on smooth wood ramps and flatground in Brooklyn, sessions emphasize patience, safe footwork, and zero ego.

Beyond indoor practice, the community coordinates weekend meetups at nearby public parks. Pair a session with a visit to Labor Skateshop on Canal Street for hardware advice, or head to Astoria Skatepark in Queens for wide open street lines.

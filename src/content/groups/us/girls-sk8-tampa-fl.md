Girls-SK8 hosts a free meet-up for girls and women at Skatepark of Tampa on the last Saturday of each month. The session starts at 11am, the park provides a free lunch at noon, and skating runs until 2:30pm. Admission is free for participants. Private group lessons at the park are not part of the free session.

The group states its position on age plainly: fun at the skatepark does not stop after 30. Its site has a section for women in their 30s, 40s, 50s and beyond who want to encourage each other to move past their comfort zone. First-timers are welcome, and you can come once or every month.

The site also mentions meet-ups in St. Pete and Sarasota, but every date we found was at Skatepark of Tampa, so check the site before you travel. See the [Skatepark of Tampa](/directory/parks/us/skatepark-of-tampa-fl) listing for the park itself.

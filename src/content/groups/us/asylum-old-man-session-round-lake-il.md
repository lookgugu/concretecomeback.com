Asylum Skatepark is an indoor park at 388 S Curran Road in Round Lake, north of Chicago. Its rates page lists an Old Man Session on Wednesdays for guests over 30 at 8 dollars. The standard day rate is 20 dollars for non-members and 10 dollars for members, so the park is giving older skaters a clear reason to pick one day and come together.

Because it is indoors, the session works in a Midwest winter when the outdoor parks are closed. Bring valid ID, as the park checks age for this rate.

One point is not clear from the site. The same rate also appears under the park's summer specials as the Old Man Skate Jam, all day every Wednesday, "all summer long". Call the park on (847) 578-5103 to confirm the Wednesday rate in other seasons before you drive out. The park is also open to BMX.

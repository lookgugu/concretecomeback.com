Commonwealth Skateboarding closes its indoor park to everyone under 30 for two hours each Wednesday night. The park's own description is short: the session is for riders 30 and over, and it exists to give parents a safe space to keep skating. The retail shop stays open to all ages during the session.

If you are coming back to a board after years away, this is the easiest way into the Portland scene. You skate with people at the same stage of life, the park is indoors so rain does not cancel the session, and nobody is watching from the deck with a phone. Fill out the waiver online before you arrive. See the [Commonwealth Skateboarding](/directory/parks/us/commonwealth-skateboarding-portland-or) park listing for the layout, and [Skate Like a Girl Portland](/directory/groups/us/skate-like-a-girl-portland-or) for more coached sessions in the city.

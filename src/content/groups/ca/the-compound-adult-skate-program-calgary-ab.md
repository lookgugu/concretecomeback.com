The Compound, at 4303 9th St SE in Calgary, reserves its indoor park for skaters 18 and over on Tuesday nights. The program addresses three groups by name: people who used to skate and want to get back into it, people who still skate and want pointers, and people who have never stood on a board.

Each night has a fixed structure. It starts with a 40-minute warm-up and instruction in groups set by ability. Then there are 40 minutes of free skating with coaches on hand, and a cool-down and recap to finish. All zones of the park are open during the session, including the beginner area, the street section, the bowl and the flatground.

This is a paid program. It runs in blocks of four to six weeks, and a single drop-in session is also available. Dates and prices change by season, so check the program page. A skateboard and helmet are mandatory at the park, and every skater must complete a waiver. For outdoor sessions in the city, see [Cowboys Park Skatepark](/directory/parks/ca/cowboys-park-calgary-ab).

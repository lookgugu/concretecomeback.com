Junction Skate and BMX Park is at 387 Riversdale Road, Hawthorn East, and is run by the YMCA. On Sundays at 2pm its coaches run a one-hour group session for adults. The park's pitch is direct: if you always wanted to learn to skateboard and thought you had missed your chance, think again. The sessions are for every age and experience level.

A session costs 20 Australian dollars and all gear is provided, so you can try it before you buy a board. The park describes the goal as skills first and community second: come for the skills, stay for the people.

The group sessions stop for the winter months of June, July and August. In rain, or when it is hotter than 33 degrees Celsius, the session is called off and you can take a refund or move to the next week. The park's phone is 0459 988 125. For a self-organised group of older skaters in the same city, see [Adult Skate Melbourne](/directory/groups/au/adult-skate-melbourne).

Old Skaters gives older skaters a structure to practise against. A season lasts eight weeks, and each week has one trick to learn. The site lists seven seasons so far, plus off-season programs for the months between them.

This suits a skater who has no local crew, or who skates alone at quiet hours and wants a reason to keep going back. You work on the same trick as other amateur skaters over 30 in the same week, wherever you live.

The site does not state a price on its home page, so check before you sign up. For open discussion without a program, see [Skaters Over 40](/directory/groups/online/skaters-over-40).

London Skate Mums began in 2020 as a small WhatsApp chat and now has more than 100 members. The group's own line on Instagram is short: all ages, all abilities, all raising kids. Huck magazine, which joined a session at the Southbank Undercroft, calls the club's greatest success the fact that it gives middle-aged women with children a place to show up and belong at a skate spot.

There are three kinds of session. Monday nights are weekly meet-ups at a beginner-friendly indoor skatepark, with a focus on progression. Every two weeks there is a midday weekday session, mostly for mums at home or with flexible work. Once a month the group runs Skate Club, a coached session on skatepark etiquette, confidence and new skills.

To join, send the group a message on Instagram and ask for the WhatsApp group. Venues are shared with members, so do not travel before you have the details. For a monthly evening with other older skaters in west London, see [Old Man Nite at BaySixty6](/directory/groups/uk/baysixty6-old-man-nite-london).

The Skate Farm is an indoor skatepark in Haywards Heath (RH16 1TX), run as a community interest company. Every Friday evening it holds a session for skaters over 35. The park's words for it are low-pressure and no-ego, and it recommends the session as a first step before you commit to a lesson.

The same team runs private 1-to-1 lessons for adults and says it works with people from their twenties through to their sixties and beyond. Note that the park's group lessons can include younger riders, so the Friday session or a 1-to-1 is the right choice if you want to skate with older skaters only.

Sessions are booked online. The site does not list a start time or a price on the lessons page, so check the booking link before you travel. For another indoor option on the Sussex coast, see [The Skate Club Hove](/directory/groups/uk/the-skate-club-hove).

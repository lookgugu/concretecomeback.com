BaySixty6 is at Bay 66, Acklam Road, North Kensington, W10 5YU. The park is open from noon to 9pm seven days a week and stays open until 10pm for its late sessions. Old Man Nite is the Friday late session, and it is booked through the park's site.

For an older skater in London, the value is a set evening when the park is given over to people like you, after work. The park also keeps weekend mornings from 10am to noon for beginners only, which is a quieter place to start if you are new to a board.

The park's home page does not state a minimum age or a price for Old Man Nite, so confirm both when you book. You must register as a member before your first visit.

BaySixty6 is at Bay 66, Acklam Road, North Kensington, W10 5YU. The park is open from noon to 9pm seven days a week. Old Man Nite is its late session on the last Friday of each month, from 7pm to 10pm. Do not turn up on another Friday: the first Friday is Girls Night and the second Friday is Queer + Transkaters Night.

For an older skater in London, the value is a set evening each month when the park is given over to people like you, after work. The park also keeps weekend mornings from 10am to noon for beginners only, which is a quieter place to start if you are new to a board.

The session is booked through the park's site, and the park says its session times change only for private events, so check the event calendar before you travel. The park's event pages give the age rule as 30 and over. Confirm the price when you book. You must register as a member before your first visit.

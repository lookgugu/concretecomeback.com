Located in Hove on the Sussex coast, The Skate Club operates a private indoor timber skatepark with a dedicated focus on coaching and community. Their evening sessions for older skaters are designed as an explicit counterweight to crowded public parks: no shouting, no ego, and structured guidance from patient instructors who understand how to pace learning safely.

The facility features mellow micro-ramps, smooth timber banks, and forgiving flatground ideal for dialling in turns, kickturns, and balance recovery. When weather allows outdoor sessions, the group regularly visits The Level Skatepark in neighbouring Brighton.

### Section 1: Three-Column Concave Geometry Matrix (Middle-Top 30%)
- **How to Measure Strip:** Lay a rigid metal ruler across both outer rails midway between the front and rear truck holes over the grip tape and measure the vertical gap down to the deck centerline.

| Geometry & Feel Metric (8.5" Deck) | Mellow (Low) Concave | Medium (Standard) Concave | Steep (High / Full) Concave |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Center-to-Rail Drop** | `0.30"` to `0.35"` (`7.5` to `9.0mm`) | `0.38"` to `0.45"` (`9.5` to `11.5mm`) | `0.48"` to `0.55"+` (`12.0` to `14.0mm+`) |
| **Flat Center Belly Width** | `3.5"` to `4.5"` flat span | `2.5"` to `3.25"` flat span | `1.5"` to `2.25"` tub or radial curve |
| **Tail & Nose Kick Angle** | `17` to `19` degrees | `18` to `20` degrees (standard) | `20` to `22` degrees (steeper ankle arc) |
| **Sole Contact & Arch Load** | Full outsole sits nearly flat | Mild pocket rise at outer `2"` of rail | Load on 1st/5th metatarsals & outer rails |
| **45-Min+ Foot Cramping Risk** | Lowest for bowls, ramps & flat feet | Low to moderate (depends on shoe shank) | Highest for wide feet, flat arches, thin shoes |

- **Wide-Deck Multiplier Callout:** Extending a press mold from `8.25"` to `8.75"` or `9.0"` adds `0.05"` to `0.08"` of outer rail height on the exact same radius mold. An `8.75"` medium bowl deck equals the total edge rise of an `8.125"` steep street deck.

### Section 2: 5-Row Foot Cramping Differential Diagnostic (Middle-Bottom 35%)
- **Section Header:** Match Your Mid-Session Foot Symptom to the Fix

| Primary Symptom Pattern | Most Likely Culprit | Fastest Equipment or Technique Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Arch burns cross-board in a bowl, even in stiff shoes** | Steep concave (`0.48"+` drop) or deep pockets | Switch to `0.30"` to `0.38"` mellow/low-medium deck with wide flat belly |
| **Forefoot & arch ache on flat + rough ground in 15 min** | Thin vulcanized outsole & packed-out foam insole | Move to cushioned cupsoles or add `6mm` to `10mm` dense PU sport insole |
| **Cramping in toes & ball of foot on drop-ins/speed checks** | Reflexive toe-clawing inside the shoe | Splay & flatten toes on flat bottom between runs; unlace bottom 2 eyelets 1 notch |
| **Whole sole tingles & feels wooden after 20 min on asphalt** | High-frequency road buzz through metal trucks | Install `1/8"` soft rubber shock pads and step down to `87A` to `93A` wheels |
| **Sharp stab under heel on first step out of bed next morning** | Potential plantar fascia or heel fat-pad issue | Pause high-impact sessions; review podiatrist foot-care guidance for skaters 40+ |

## Phase 2: Source Extraction

### Core Insight
Standard `608` skateboard bearings use `SAE 52100` chromium steel balls and races that begin oxidizing within 4 to 6 hours when winter water and road salt (`NaCl` or magnesium chloride) wick past the non-contact labyrinth shield gap; rescuing a wet set requires popping the wheels off within 2 hours, prying the rubber shields off from the inner race edge and washing the shields separately in warm soapy water, agitating the unshielded bearings in `99%` isopropyl alcohol in a glass jar in a ventilated space away from heat or sparks, drying on a lint-free towel or with unheated compressed air, and adding 2 drops of low-viscosity skate oil per bearing.

### Supporting Points (Standalone Atoms)
1. **Why winter road brine locks 608 bearings overnight:** Even when a concrete slab looks mostly dry on a `40°F` morning, mist and snowmelt carry dissolved de-icing salts across the surface. Leaving a wet board in a warm indoor hallway speeds evaporation while chloride ions and iron oxide form an abrasive paste that locks the seven-ball nylon retainer cage against the inner raceway within 4 to 6 hours.
2. **The 6-solvent reality check (and why rubber shields stay out of the jar):** `99%` isopropyl alcohol (`1%` water) is the best all-around winter flush (2 to 3 minute shake, safe on nylon ball cages once rubber shields are removed). `70%` rubbing alcohol contains `30%` water that beads on bare steel and causes orange flash rust. Pure acetone cuts grease in 60 to 90 seconds inside a glass jar, yet softens ABS plastics and blisters rubber shields. `WD-40` displaces water in an emergency, yet strips skate oil and leaves a tacky mineral film that traps road dust unless flushed out with `99%` IPA and re-oiled.
3. **Why you pry shields from the inner race and never use a heated hairdryer:** Slipping a sewing pin under the inside edge of the rubber shield right next to the inner steel ring prevents bending the thin metal backing plate (prying from the outer groove bends the shield so it drags against the nylon cage forever). Because `99%` IPA and acetone emit flammable vapors, never use a heated hairdryer, heat gun, or brushed electric motor on solvent-wet bearings; tap bulk liquid onto a lint-free towel and use brief bursts of unheated compressed air while holding the outer ring so the dry bearing does not free-spin and score the unlubricated races.
4. **Surface flash rust vs. pitted races (4-row diagnostic):** Faint orange haze on outer faces with a quiet spin under thumb load is cosmetic flash rust (keep and skate). A slight hiss with a 10-plus-second free spin and zero hard clicks makes a dedicated winter beater set (`$18` to `$25` Bones Reds, Mini Logo, or Bronson G2). Rhythmic metallic ticking or a gritty crunch under thumb pressure means corrosion craters in the steel raceway (replace). Stopping within 2 seconds or catching at one spot signals deep cratering or a cracked nylon ball retainer finger that can shatter and lock a front wheel mid-carve (replace immediately).

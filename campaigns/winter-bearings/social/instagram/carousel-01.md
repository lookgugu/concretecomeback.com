## Slide-by-Slide Text (for `/creative` production, 1080x1350 4:5 canvas)

### Slide 1 (Cover)
- **Headline:** 20-Minute Wet Winter 608 Bearing Rescue
- **Subhead:** How to flush road salt and moisture out of wet skateboard bearings before 4-to-6-hour oxidation pits the steel races.
- **Visual note:** Dark `#141210` card with `#F59E0B` amber accent pill (`BOARD SETUP · WINTER MAINTENANCE`), top-down workbench diagram showing an unshielded 7-ball 608 bearing, a sewing pin lifting the rubber shield from the inner ring edge, a glass jar of 99% IPA, and a 2-drop skate oil bottle.

### Slide 2 (The 4-to-6-Hour Road Salt Clock)
- **Header:** Why Damp Winter Concrete Locks Bearings Overnight
- **The metallurgy:** Standard `608` bearings use `SAE 52100` chromium steel balls and races.
- **The brine trap:** Fine mist and snowmelt carry dissolved road salt (`NaCl` or magnesium chloride) past the non-contact labyrinth shield gap.
- **The indoor mistake:** Leaving a damp board in a warm hallway overnight evaporates the water within `4` to `6` hours while chloride crystals and iron oxide bind the 7-ball nylon cage to the inner race. Pop wet wheels off within `2` hours of getting home.

### Slide 3 (Solvent Comparison Matrix)
- **Header:** Which Solvents Safely Flush Wet 608 Bearings?
- **99% Isopropyl Alcohol (`1%` water):** Best all-around winter flush · `2` to `3` min shake · safe on nylon cages once rubber shields are removed.
- **91% Isopropyl Alcohol (`9%` water):** Solid pharmacy backup · `3` to `4` min shake · dry promptly with unheated air.
- **70% Rubbing Alcohol (`30%` water):** Avoid · `30%` water beads on bare steel and triggers orange flash rust.
- **Pure Acetone (`~0%` water):** `60` to `90` sec shake in a glass jar only · keep rubber shields and ABS spacers out.
- **WD-40 Spray:** Water displacer rather than a bearing lubricant · leaves a tacky film that traps dust unless stripped with `99%` IPA and re-oiled.

### Slide 4 (Steps 1 & 2: Axle Lever & Inner-Edge Shield Removal)
- **Header:** Keep Rubber Shields Out of the Solvent Jar
- **1. Lever on the truck axle tip:** Back off the `1/2"` axle nuts, hook only the inner steel ring on the rounded axle tip, and tilt the wheel firmly until the bearing pops free. Wipe trapped water out of the urethane wheel seat.
- **2. Pry shields from the INNER race edge:** Slip a straight sewing pin shallowly under the inside lip next to the inner steel ring and flick upward. Prying from the outer groove bends the thin steel backing plate so it drags forever.
- **3. Wash shields separately:** Solvents swell and blister bonded rubber lips. Wash the 8 rubber shields in warm soapy water and pat dry on a paper towel.

### Slide 5 (Steps 3, 4 & 5: Glass Jar Flush, Unheated Dry & 2-Drop Oil)
- **Header:** Flush, Dry Safe, and Add Exactly 2 Drops
- **3. Two-minute glass jar shake:** In a ventilated room away from heaters or sparks, shake the 8 unshielded bearings in `99%` IPA for `2` minutes, pour off the gray grit, and do a `30`-second clean alcohol rinse.
- **4. Unheated drying only:** Tap bulk liquid onto a lint-free towel. Never use a heated hairdryer or heat gun on flammable alcohol or acetone vapors. Use brief bursts of unheated compressed air while holding the outer ring so the dry bearing does not free-spin and score the unlubricated races.
- **5. Two drops of light skate oil:** Place `2` drops of low-viscosity skate oil per bearing, spin the outer ring `10` times by hand, press dry shields flat, and snug axle nuts to `0.5mm` of side play.

### Slide 6 (Surface Flash Rust vs. Pitted Races Diagnostic)
- **Header:** After the Flush: Keep or Replace?
- **Faint orange tint on faces + quiet spin under thumb load:** Superficial flash rust outside the ball track · **Keep and skate** (re-check oil after 3 sessions).
- **Slight hiss + 10s+ free spin + zero hard clicks:** Minor surface etching on balls with intact races · **Keep as your dedicated winter beater set.**
- **Rhythmic metallic ticking or gritty crunch under thumb load:** Corrosion pits in the hardened steel raceway · **Replace the pitted bearing.**
- **Stops within 2 seconds or catches at one spot:** Deep cratering or cracked nylon retainer finger · **Replace immediately** before the cage shatters and locks a wheel mid-carve.

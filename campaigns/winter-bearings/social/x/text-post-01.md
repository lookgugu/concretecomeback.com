## Post Body (268 chars)

Never soak rubber 608 bearing shields in isopropyl alcohol or acetone, and never clean wet bearings with 70% rubbing alcohol.

Solvents swell and blister the bonded rubber lip so it drags on the cage, while 30% water in 70% alcohol flash-rusts bare SAE 52100 steel.

## Reply 1 (Link Drop)

Wash rubber shields in warm soapy water, flush the unshielded bearings in 99% isopropyl alcohol inside a glass jar, dry without heat, and add 2 drops of light skate oil. Full winter bearing rescue guide for older skaters: https://concretecomeback.com/blog/cleaning-skateboard-bearings-damp-winter-sessions/

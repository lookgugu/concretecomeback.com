### Section 1: Six-Solvent Comparison Table (Middle-Top 30%)

| Solvent or Spray | Water Content | Rubber Shields & Nylon Cage Rule | Flash-Rust Risk | Workbench Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **99% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA)** | `1%` | Safe on nylon cage; wash rubber shields in soapy water | Very low (`2` to `3` min shake) | Best all-around winter flush |
| **91% Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA)** | `9%` | Safe on nylon cage; remove rubber shields first | Low if dried promptly with unheated air | Good pharmacy backup |
| **70% Rubbing Alcohol** | `30%` | Safe on nylon cage; remove rubber shields first | High (water beads on bare steel) | Avoid; causes orange flash rust |
| **Pure Acetone (100%)** | `~0%` | Brief rinse in glass jar only; softens ABS & blisters rubber | Very low (`60` to `90` sec shake) | Fast grease cutter; glass jar only |
| **Citrus Degreaser** | Water rinse | Requires full water rinse after soaking | High in damp winter weather | Avoid for winter moisture rescue |
| **WD-40 Multi-Use Spray** | `0%` | Displaces water; dissolves skate oil and leaves tacky film | Moderate after carriers evaporate | Emergency flush only; strip with IPA & re-oil |

### Section 2: 5-Step 20-Minute Wet-Session Rescue (Middle 30%)
1. **Pop bearings on the axle tip within 2 hours:** Hook only the inner steel ring on the rounded truck axle end and wipe trapped water out of the urethane wheel seat.
2. **Pry rubber shields from the INNER race edge:** Slip a sewing pin under the inner lip next to the inner steel ring (outer-groove prying bends the metal backing plate). Wash shields separately in warm soapy water.
3. **Agitate 2 minutes in 99% IPA in a glass jar:** Shake in a ventilated space away from heaters or sparks, pour off cloudy grit, and finish with a `30`-second clean alcohol rinse.
4. **Dry without heat:** Tap bulk liquid onto a lint-free towel or use brief bursts of unheated compressed air while holding the outer ring (never use a heated hairdryer on flammable vapors or spin dry bearings at high RPM).
5. **Apply 2 drops of light skate oil:** Coat the 7 balls, spin the outer ring `10` times by hand, press dry shields flat, and snug axle nuts to `0.5mm` of side play.

### Section 3: Surface Flash Rust vs. Pitted Races & Footer (Bottom 25%)

| Symptom After Flush & 2 Drops of Oil | Inside the 608 Bearing | Keep or Replace? |
|---|---|---|
| **Faint orange tint on faces; quiet spin under load** | Superficial flash rust outside ball track | **Keep & skate** (re-check oil after 3 sessions) |
| **Slight hiss; 10s+ free spin; zero hard clicks** | Minor surface etching on balls; races intact | **Keep as a dedicated winter beater set** |
| **Rhythmic metallic ticking or gritty crunch** | Corrosion pits in hardened steel raceway | **Replace pitted bearing** |
| **Stops in <2 seconds or catches at one spot** | Deep cratering or cracked nylon cage finger | **Replace immediately** (wheel-lock risk) |

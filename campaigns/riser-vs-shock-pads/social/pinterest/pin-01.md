### Section 1: Two-Column Mechanical Comparison (Middle-Top 25%)
- **Left Card: Hard Plastic Risers (`1/8"`, `1/4"`, `1/2"`):**
  - Material: Rigid polypropylene or nylon
  - Bolt compression: Zero (holds fixed axle-to-deck gap)
  - Job: Stop wheel bite on `56mm` to `60mm+` wheels
  - Best for: Bowl and cruiser setups (`56mm`–`60mm`)
- **Right Card: Soft Rubber Shock Pads (`1/8"` / `3.2mm`):**
  - Material: Soft rubber or `80A`–`90A` polyurethane
  - Bolt compression: `~0.5mm`–`1.0mm` under locknuts
  - Job: Filter road buzz & stop bolt-hole pressure cracks
  - Best for: Rough asphalt, crust, and park setups (`53mm`–`56mm`)

### Section 2: Wheel Diameter × Rider Weight Matrix (Middle-Bottom 35%)
- **Table Callout Note:** Moving from `52mm` to `56mm` wheels reduces deck clearance by `2mm` (`0.5mm` radius per `1mm` diameter). Riders at `175`–`220+ lbs` compress bushings deeper and hit wheel bite `~2mm` earlier than lightweight charts show.

| Wheel Size | Under 175 lbs (Mid/High Trucks) | 175–220+ lbs (Mid/High Trucks) | Hardware Bolts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **52mm – 53mm** | No pad (or `1/8"` shock pad) | No pad (or `1/8"` shock pad) | `7/8"` or `1"` (`1"` w/ pad) |
| **54mm – 55mm** | No pad (or `1/8"` shock pad) | `1/8"` pad (unless `94A+` bushings) | `1"` or `1 1/8"` |
| **56mm – 57mm** | `1/8"` shock pad or riser | `1/8"` riser + `94A+` bushings, or `1/4"` riser | `1 1/8"` (`1/8"`) · `1 1/4"` (`1/4"`) |
| **58mm – 60mm+** | `1/4"` hard riser or wheel wells | `1/4"` hard riser + wheel wells | `1 1/4"` (`1/4"`) · `1 1/2"` (`1/2"`) |

### Section 3: Installation Callout Strip & Footer (Bottom 25%)
- **Bolt Fit Quick Rule:** Bare deck = `7/8"`–`1"` · `1/8"` pad = `1"` min / `1 1/8"` easy-start on new rubber · `1/4"` pad = `1 1/4"` · `1/2"` pad = `1 1/2"`.
- **Two Shop Rules:** Do not stack rubber on plastic (bolts flex and shear under side-load). Tighten rubber pads until bolt heads sit flush with grip tape without crushing the `1/8"` cushion flat.

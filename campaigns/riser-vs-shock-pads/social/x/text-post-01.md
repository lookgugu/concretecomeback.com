A 1/8" (3.2mm) rubber shock pad stops high-frequency asphalt buzz from numbing your feet after 20 minutes on rough pavement.

Once four bolts pre-compress 3.2mm of rubber, it cannot absorb heavy drop force. Wheels, cupsoles, and knee bend take the landing.
